## Key Observations

- **Custom CUDA vs CPU**: Up to **523x** speedup (3D 64³ with 500 splats)
- **Peak throughput**: **46.9 GV/s** (AMP, 3D 768³ with 50K splats)
- **AMP benefits**: Most pronounced at medium-to-large workloads (256³-512³ with 2K-20K splats), up to 2.35x over FP32
- **FP16 inference**: Competitive with FP32, occasionally faster (up to 1.55x at 512³ 20K)
- **AMP training**: Best gains at 384³-512³ range (1.24x-1.37x speedup), slight regression at very large (768³) or very small configs
- **Backward pass**: Uses Per-Tile Gradient Accumulation (16x fewer atomics)
- **GPU saturation**: Throughput plateaus around 512³+ volumes, indicating GPU is well-utilized

## Notes

- `-` indicates skipped (too slow or OOM for that backend)
- AMP is recommended for training (FP32 params, FP16 compute)
- FP16 is inference only (may overflow during training)
