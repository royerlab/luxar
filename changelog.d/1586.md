#### A whole *Drosophila* brain as Gaussian splats (FlyLight MCFO, 63x)

A new gsplat demo, `gsplats_3d_flylight_mcfo_63x_brain`: a female central brain
plus both optic lobes, MultiColor FlpOut-labelled, as 418,722 splats against a
2573 x 2707 x 463 confocal mosaic that is 99.97% empty — about 7,700:1. This is
the shape splats are for; the empty 99.97% costs nothing.

Assembled from the ten raw 63x LSM tiles rather than Janelia's stitched product,
to keep the full 16-bit dynamic range (the stitched H5J is 8-bit HEVC). The raw
tiles carry no stage coordinates — `Positions`, `TilePositions` and `OriginX/Y/Z`
are all zero — so placement is recovered by registering each tile against the
stitched H5J, which serves purely as a coordinate reference; its lossy voxels
never enter the output.

MCFO is a stochastic label, so a neuron's hue is the *ratio* of three channels at
the same voxels. The fit therefore runs once, on the gain-balanced per-voxel
maximum, and each splat samples its colour at its own centre. Three independent
per-channel fits would not co-locate and every axon would render candy-striped.
The nc82 reference channel is excluded: at 10-19% occupancy it is not sparse and
would dominate the fit.

Thin neurites still ghost where tiles overlap, and the module docstring says so
with the measured numbers. Residuals in four sub-blocks per overlapping pair split
into two regimes: five pairs vary by 4.2-7.5 voxels across a single overlap, so no
per-tile translation can align them, and two are near-constant but offset by 9-13
voxels (rigidly correctable, and not corrected here). Janelia's own pipeline
applies a non-rigid distortion correction for exactly this reason; removing the
seams properly needs a piecewise fit.
