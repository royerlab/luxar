#### Preserve fitting metadata when reencoding archives

`luxar gsplat reencode` now carries the input's fitting statistics, fit
configuration, provenance, and pipeline metadata through `.zip` and `.tar.gz`
inputs. Archive rewrites previously produced valid-looking splats while silently
dropping those groups from the output store. Root-stat loading now also includes
`fitting/config`, so stats-based rewrites preserve that nested configuration.
