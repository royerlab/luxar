#### A tiled fit scores the archive it actually ships

Every tile of a tiled fit scores itself, but the tiles overlap and are
Hann-apodized, so their errors do not compose into the merged one — and the
merged result is what gets written. A tiled archive therefore carried no
`psnr_db` and no `foreground_psnr_db` at all, which is precisely what a
published dataset is asked to state.

`fit_tiled` now renders the merged reconstruction once against the whole volume
it still has in hand and stamps the metrics. When the fit returned physical
coordinates the merged splats are first mapped back onto the tile grid: the two
frames differ by one per-axis factor that scales centers directly and Cholesky
row `i` by `scale[i]`, so dividing both inverts it exactly. That inverse is the
part worth testing, and it is: the same fit scored with and without the
conversion must agree, which a mutation collapses from 46 dB to 25 dB.

Scoring needs the reconstruction and the reference resident together, so it is
capped (24 GiB by default, `LUXAR_TILED_QUALITY_MAX_GB` to raise it) and says so
out loud when it declines — an archive that silently carries no PSNR is the
failure this exists to end. Two cases are deliberately left unscored and say
why: a `kind=partition` result, which has nowhere to persist fit stats, and the
parallel worker path, where the workers hold the volume rather than the parent.
`luxar gsplat compare` remains the recourse for both.
