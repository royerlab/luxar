#### A tiled fit scores the archive it actually ships

Every tile of a tiled fit scores itself, but the tiles overlap and are
Hann-apodized, so their errors do not compose into the merged one — and the
merged result is what gets written. A tiled archive therefore carried no
`psnr_db` and no `foreground_psnr_db` at all, which is precisely what a
published dataset is asked to state.

`fit_tiled` now renders the merged reconstruction once against the whole volume
it still has in hand and stamps the metrics. When the fit returned physical
coordinates the merged splats are first mapped back onto the tile grid: the two
frames differ by one per-axis factor that scales centers directly and Cholesky
row `i` by `scale[i]`, so dividing both inverts it exactly. That inverse is the
part worth testing, and it is: the same fit scored with and without the
conversion must agree, which a mutation collapses from 46 dB to 25 dB.

Scoring is capped because it runs *before* the archive is written, so an
over-budget score does not cost a metric — it costs the whole fit. The default
budget is half the memory actually free, held under a 24 GiB ceiling, so a fit on
a laptop is not licensed by a constant that describes a workstation;
`LUXAR_TILED_QUALITY_MAX_GB` overrides both (`0` declines scoring outright, and a
value that cannot be read falls back with a note rather than taking the run
down). The cap is measured against the real peak, which sits inside SSIM rather
than at the render: the reconstruction, the reference, and the convolution
intermediates SSIM keeps live, whose own tiled fallback engages on CUDA only.
When the cap declines, or the score fails,
it says so out loud even under `--quiet` — an archive that silently carries no
PSNR is the failure this exists to end. Two cases are deliberately left unscored
and say why, printing under `--quiet` for the same reason: a `kind=partition`
merge, whose tree node has no fit-stats dict to stamp and which this path saves
without threading any through, and the parallel worker path, which is handed only
the tile grid's shape and not the volume. `luxar gsplat compare` is the recourse
for both — by way of `luxar gsplat flatten` on a partition, which `compare`
cannot load directly. Content-adaptive tiling (`--tiling content`) goes through
the planner and is untouched here; it still stamps no merged metric.
