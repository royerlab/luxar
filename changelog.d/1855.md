#### Native scene partitions retain their BSP split planes

`add_points`, `add_lines`, `add_mesh`, and `add_gsplats` now store the recursive
split planes used by `partition=` on the wrapper as `bsp_tree`. The viewer can
therefore traverse freshly compiled parts in stable BSP back-to-front order
instead of falling back to whole-part centroid sorting, which could pop at part
seams as the camera moved. The planes exactly separate points and splats; lines
and mesh are split by polyline or face centroid, so their vertex bounds may cross
a plane and the order is correspondingly approximate. Trees are pruned and
renumbered against the parts actually written, so an empty region cannot leave a
valid-looking tree pointing at the wrong child.
