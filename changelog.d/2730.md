#### Playback detail: pin the ladder depth for smooth, consistent time-lapse playback

Dimension playback streamed each frame under a per-tick time budget: a frame showed
whatever additive-ladder rungs were resident within its tick and the background prefetch
deepened the next frame by roughly one rung per tick. On a heavy time-lapse -- millions
of splats per timepoint -- most frames therefore landed at their first one or two rungs
and quality flickered from frame to frame. The dimension context menu gains a **Detail**
section that pins the ladder depth: every frame is drawn at exactly that many rungs, cold
or not, and the tick waits for the data, so quality stays constant and the frame rate
adapts instead. The default, **Auto**, lets each ladder pick its depth from its own
`energy_fraction_cum` build stamps (the first rung at or above a 0.9 energy fraction);
a rung count or **All** pins it explicitly, and **Fast** keeps the old time-budgeted
streaming. Scrubbing is pinned the same way, with an unpinned refine once the scrub is
quiet. Scenes can author the default with `ViewerConfig(playback_lod_depth=...)`
(`int`, `"all"`, `"auto"` or `"fast"`), stored as `viewer_config.playback_lod_depth`. The
pin rides the same per-pass directive path as the frame budget (`ViewState.ladderDepth`),
reaches the t+1 shadow prefetch, and is available programmatically as
`play(dim, { ladderDepth })` / `setLadderDepth` / `setDefaultLadderDepth`. Applies to all
four geometry loaders.
