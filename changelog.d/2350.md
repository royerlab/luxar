#### Zenodo characteristic refreshes stop reporting unreadable archives as success (#2350)

`gen_zenodo_records.py --refresh` now writes every successful measurement before reporting pinned fit archives it could not read and exiting non-zero. Unreadable copies whose bytes do not match the pin are counted separately without failing, while non-fit companion files are ignored. Explicit `--archives-root` runs report how many archive candidates were selected from that staging tree and warn when none matched, including the namespaced layout the command expects.
