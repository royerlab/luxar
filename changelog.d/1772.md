#### Mesh shading gains an offset key light and specular controls

Mesh surfaces now use an above-left view-space key light and a subtle default
specular highlight. Five mesh-only appearance controls become authorable for the
first time — `specular`, `shininess`, `ambient`, `shade_exponent`, and
`alpha_cutoff` — settable on a mesh node (in authored scenes and the Layers
panel) and refused on non-mesh nodes.

Existing standalone exports bundle their viewer build and retain the previous
shading until they are re-exported.
