#### DPR-invariant point, line, and Gaussian-splat appearance

Screen-space anti-aliasing thresholds now stay in CSS-pixel units when the
render-target scale changes. Point and line minimum footprints, line-join and
capsule gates, and Gaussian-splat covariance dilation scale with DPR, SSAA, and
the picking target in matching GLSL and WebGPU/TSL paths, while preserving the
historical framebuffer-pixel floor below 1×.
