#### Add ambient occlusion to the 4D fractals demo

The 4D fractals demo now bakes normal-aware ambient occlusion independently
for every fractal and w slice, adding view-independent depth in concavities
without hidden slices darkening the visible one. Its display window and gallery
framing are retuned for the shaded 147 MB scene; the default build takes about
25 minutes and peaks near 24 GB of memory.
