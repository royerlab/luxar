#### Slow streaming chunks no longer escape the shared fetch bound (#2642)

HTTP chunk responses now keep their shared fetch-gate slot until the body is
consumed or cancelled, so a congested connection cannot accumulate an
unbounded set of streaming bodies behind the nominal 64-request limit. Header
waits and body no-progress stalls retain separate retry windows, while an
absolute body deadline derived from `Content-Length` at a 16 KiB/s floor (or
eight stall windows without a usable length) prevents a trickling response from
pinning a slot forever.

The stricter bound means metadata and validation requests share the same FIFO
queue with full chunk bodies and can wait longer under saturation. Caller abort
reasons remain intact on browsers without `AbortSignal.any`, unread bodies are
cancelled before release, and a response body can only be consumed once.
