#### Streaming ladder budgets now describe the visible slice

Budget-derived additive ladders now scale their first rung by hidden-slice
cardinality and by the number of partition parts rendered together, rather than
sizing every leaf against the whole node independently. `gsplat lod`, `gsplat
additive`, and batch merge planning therefore produce the requested visible
first-paint payload for sliced data; fit-time per-part sizing is refused when
the final non-empty part count is not yet knowable, with `--n-lods` as the safe
alternative. The demo ladder gate now measures rung 0 globally by hidden
coordinate across partition parts and fails undersized or empty slice surveys.
