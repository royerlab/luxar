### Fixed

- The automatic GPU-geometry byte budget now derives directly from the shared non-cache heap remainder instead of inheriting the eager loader's unrelated 512 MiB burst cap. Roomy Chromium heaps therefore keep scaling (a 4 GiB heap resolves to about 1.37 GB) until the module's existing 2 GB ceiling applies; the eager-loader and refinement-residency caps remain unchanged.
- Explicit cache-pool overrides and the mobile WebKit device-class fallback now use that same remainder derivation. The native launcher's 2048 MiB default implies about 1.43 GB of GPU geometry rather than 716 MB; this is derived from the configured pool split, equivalent to the remainder from roughly a 4.2 GiB heap under the same model, rather than measured at that budget. Mobile devices still keep their independent 128 MiB safety cap.
