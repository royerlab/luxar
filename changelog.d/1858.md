#### Content-planned partitions publish merged quality metrics

The default `gsplat fit --tiling content` partition output now records whole-volume `psnr_db`, `ssim`, `mse`, and foreground quality metrics at the archive root in both sequential and parallel modes. Scoring renders and sums the surviving core-disjoint box parts, matching their additive reconstruction without flattening the splats, and retains the existing memory-budget decline diagnostics.
