#### Visible Human and CT atlas hosted pairs move as one generation (#2334)

Both demos read a row-indexed sidecar alongside their fit, so the restructured
archives from #2333 could not be published until freshly sampled colour and
label payloads existed for the new splat order. The hosted pins for
`vh_head.gsplats.zarr.zip`/`vh_head_colors.npz` and
`ct_atlas.gsplats.zarr.zip`/`ct_atlas_labels.npz` now advance together, each
retaining its outgoing digest in `superseded_sha256` so a warm cache is
recognised as out of date rather than corrupt, and the two archive
characteristic rows are refreshed to describe the restructured ladders.
