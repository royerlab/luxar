#### Viewer: fly mode works by touch

Fly mode listened only to mouse events, so on a phone or tablet one of the
three camera modes did nothing at all. It now understands the same fingers the
orbit mode does: one finger looks around, two fingers dragged together strafe,
a pinch flies forward or back along the view direction (pinch-in exactly undoes
pinch-out), and a two-finger twist rolls. The gestures drive the same
velocity and orientation state as the mouse, so inertia and damping feel
identical, and a touch press no longer produces the synthetic mouse events that
would have dragged the camera sideways under every tap. Mouse behaviour is
unchanged.
