#### Store the neuromast rebuild in physical microns

The two-channel neuromast rebuild recipe now scales `(Z, Y, X)` by the measured
MetaMorph voxel size `(0.25, 0.1083, 0.1083)` µm instead of applying only the
Z-to-lateral anisotropy ratio. Rebuilt axis readouts and measured distances now
match the `µm` labels; scene dimensions also cover the union of both channels'
quantized bounds. This supersedes the anisotropy-only recipe correction recorded
separately for the pending archive repin.

The archives currently pinned in the demo manifest are unchanged and still use
the historical Z-only transform. Their coordinates remain in lateral-pixel
units, with the separate 8.3 % Z stretch tracked by the pending archive repin.
