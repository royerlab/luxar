#### Playback feedback now says whether the frames are actually thin (#2393)

The dimension-animation feedback warned whenever measured cadence fell below 80%
of the requested rate. Cadence alone cannot say whether anything is wrong, and
after #2377 it usually means the opposite of a problem: a playback pass now
streams every cache-resident ladder rung, so the playhead slows to wait for data
— the pacing gate working as designed — and the warning fires on healthy
playback. Before #2377 the same signal was silent right through the blank-frame
defect in #2374, because a pass committed one rung and stopped, so cadence was
*met* while the screen showed 40 of 166,443 splats. A channel that stays quiet
when the picture is empty and shouts when it is complete teaches a reader to
ignore it.

The feedback now consults committed quality. `worstCommittedEnergy` walks the
visible, non-empty commit stamps and returns the lowest
`committedEnergyFraction` — a minimum rather than a mean, because one node
painting fully does not excuse another painting thin, and it is the thinnest
thing on screen that a viewer notices. A cadence slip or energy below
`ENERGY_RELEASE_THRESHOLD` (0.6, reused from the display gate rather than
invented) emits feedback. Below the energy threshold the warning says the frame
is still filling in, even if cadence is met; at or above it a cadence slip is
informational and says the playhead is pacing to data with enough on screen to
read. The `fpsWarning` event carries the figure either way, so a future consumer
gets it without re-deriving.

A scene carrying no energy stamps reports `null`, which means "cannot tell" and
falls back to the historical warning rather than assuming the frames are fine.

Two sibling follow-ups from #2374 were **measured and dropped** rather than
built. Holding the previous frame across a slice change is no longer warranted:
a fast scrub now holds 14.2% of the frame where it once fell to 0.03%, and on a
time axis holding a stale frame shows the wrong moment, which is worse than a
degraded current one. Protecting coarse rungs from cache eviction is not
warranted either: over 25 s of playback the coarse rungs took 15 requests
against the fine rungs' 193, with an L0 hit rate of 0.934, so they are already
staying resident.
