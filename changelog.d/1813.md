#### Reject unsupported content-batch fit options

Content batch planning now rejects on-the-fly `--denoise` and `--progressive` instead of emitting content-box tasks that silently ignore them. `batch-fit submit --preprocess` remains supported because it denoises to a store before the boxes fit. Direct `fit --tiling content` invocations keep their warned-and-ignored behavior, including hand-run `--plan-box` workers and the explicit `--downscale` flag; a `downscale:` in the fit config remains supported by the content fitter.
