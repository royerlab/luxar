#### Failed wrapper writes no longer strand partial scene state

Scene geometry additions are now transactional: a failure after a partition or
LOD wrapper starts writing removes the new subtree and restores scene children,
bounds, label flags, warnings, and array-reference deduplication state.
Finalization warns and prunes childless wrapper chains as a structural backstop.
The exported `ZarrWriterProtocol` consequently gains a public
`transaction(path)` context-manager contract for writer implementations.
