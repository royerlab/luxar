#### Additive LOD ladders are visible in the viewer debug surface (#2373)

`window.__luxarDebug` now reports bounded per-level additive load timings,
per-node committed ladder depth and quality, a sampled presented-frame rate,
and SliceCache ladder-depth/fullness statistics. This makes nD playback
quality and cache convergence directly observable without reconstructing them
from raw element counts or renderer pass counters.
