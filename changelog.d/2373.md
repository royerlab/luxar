#### Additive LOD ladders are visible in the viewer debug surface (#2373)

`window.__luxarDebug` now reports bounded per-level additive load timings,
per-node committed ladder depth and quality, the adaptive-DPR healthy-cadence
estimate plus whether its sampler is active, and SliceCache ladder-depth/fullness
statistics. Mesh reveal ladders also show residency in the data monitor's
scene-graph row. This makes nD playback quality and cache convergence directly
observable without reconstructing them from raw element counts or renderer pass
counters; the cadence estimate rejects isolated stalls and is not perceived FPS.
