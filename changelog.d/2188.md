#### Dimension navigation labels stay on one line

The Dimension Navigation panel now keeps its title and row labels on one line
when datasets use long categorical values. The compact header reports only the
keyboard target and displayed axes, preserves its full text in a tooltip, and
ellipsizes safely when space is tight; individual controls remain the canonical
value readout and retain units for compact categorical pickers. A responsive
height cap also keeps common three- and four-dimension control sets visible
without sacrificing small-window bounds.
