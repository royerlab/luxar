#### A written ground truth for the viewer's visual design

The viewer's design language — the "quiet instrument" — lived in people's heads
and in a handful of exemplary files (the control rail, the data-loading
monitor's end-of-file "Refinement layer" block). Everything else was
reconstructed by reading whichever panel happened to be nearby, which is how a
green scrollbar and a solid-green "interactive" slider became precedent.

`docs/guides/developer/UI_DESIGN_GUIDE.md` writes it down and declares itself
authoritative: the 87 design tokens and their traps (the spacing key is *half*
the pixel value; `--luxar-blur-*` values are complete `blur()` functions; there
is no `--luxar-radius-xs`), what differs between the four themes, the
glass-surface system and the five hard constraints that break a theme when
violated, color semantics (`highlight` is interactive, green is *healthy*), the
surface/header/scroll recipes, typography, the two icon contracts, motion,
placement, interaction states and a11y, naming, and a checklist for a new
surface.

Sections 15.1–15.5 are a verified inventory of the code that still contradicts
the guide, so nobody mistakes debt for a pattern. The five modernization
tranches they cataloged have since landed — a11y hardening (#1476),
emoji→stroke icons (#1479), the dataset browser (#1472), the accent migration
plus the shared panel-header/close recipes (#1480) and token hygiene (#1478) —
leaving a shorter but not merely cosmetic list.

Two surfaces still mix raw values into their chrome: the recording
start-confirm dialog and the scene-identity banner. A parallel
`config.ui.zIndex` scale in TypeScript interleaves with the z-index tokens, so
the guide tabulates the two side by side and says which one actually decides
what paints over what. The rest is the raw `rgba()` and `color: white` the
token sweep did not reach, two hand-rolled copies of the shared header and
close recipes that have since drifted from their originals, seven focus rings
spelled without the mandatory fallback, the resolution indicator's opacity ramp
on a glass root, sub-AA contrast on the glass themes' micro-label ranks, and one
modal that doesn't yet trap focus.

§15.6 is the opposite list. It registers the deliberate exceptions —
sanctioned literals, the three `!important` categories, the off-tier `calc()`
z-indexes — so nobody migrates one away and deletes the record with it.
