#### Stream `gsplat flatten` without materializing the full tree

`luxar gsplat flatten` now reads one default-selected leaf at a time and stages
the flat output on disk, removing the whole-tree load and concatenation that
made large partitioned stores run out of memory. The command requires precision
encoding; this preserves the bounded-memory write but produces larger output
than the previous auto-encoded default. Stacked categorical axes remain grouped
globally so per-timepoint chunk reads retain the locality of the in-memory path.
