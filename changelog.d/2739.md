#### Neuromast opens from above with validated channel windows

The two-channel neuromast demo now uses the amplitude windows validated against
the pinned hosted archives instead of mistaking the Layers-panel slider bounds
for display maxima. Its opening camera is derived from the amplitude-weighted
middle frame, looking down from the low-depth side at the rosette rather than
fitting the mostly empty acquisition box side-on; the data-derived pose scales
with the corrected archive geometry instead of baking world coordinates.
