#### `import luxar.validation.types` works, and ~40 dead typing names are gone

`luxar/__init__.py` bound the name `validation` to `validation/base.py` rather
than to the subpackage, so `import luxar.validation.types` raised
`ImportError: cannot import name 'types' from 'luxar.validation.base'` — and the
same for six other submodules. Internal code never hit it, because
`from luxar.validation.x import y` goes through the import system rather than the
attribute, and neither did the docs, because Sphinx resolves via `sys.modules`.
Only the form a user would reach for was broken. A second re-export existed
beside it purely as the escape hatch, with no readers at all; both are gone and
the subpackage is bound directly.

`validate_node_type()` rejected `"mesh"`. It checked against a hand-copied tuple
of five `NodeType` values where the enum has six, so a public validator refused a
first-class geometry type that the contract-reading validator next door accepted.
The node-type and physical-unit vocabularies are now derived from their enums,
which removes the last two of four separate copies of the unit list.

`luxar.typing_utils.config` is deleted. Three of its ~30 public names had a
reader: two were aliases of constants that already existed, and the third moved
next to its single caller. `typing_utils.protocols` loses the six names nothing
referenced, and `typing_utils.constants` loses thirteen — including a duplicated
point-count pair that disagreed with its twin, and a compression-level trio that
agreed with neither the real codec policy nor the validator that used to sit
above it.
