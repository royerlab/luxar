#### Changelog entries are filed under the month they were written

At release-prep the fold used to put every pending fragment under whichever
month it happened to run in. With 440 fragments waiting across two months, that
would have mis-dated 408 of them into a single unnavigable heading. Each entry
now goes under the month of the commit that added it, and a fragment git cannot
date fails the fold rather than being filed under the wrong month — committing
it is the fix, and `--month` remains the explicit override.

`make changelog-release` is new: it renames `## [Unreleased]` to
`## [<version>] - <date>` and opens a fresh empty one, so `CHANGELOG.md`
accumulates release history instead of a single ever-growing section. The
version comes from `__version__`, and the date is derived from it when it looks
like a CalVer date, so the header cannot contradict itself when the cut runs on
a different day from the version bump. It refuses while fragments are still
pending, because folding after a cut would ship those entries under the next
version.
