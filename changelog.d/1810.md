#### Expand demo credits and gallery coverage

Thirty-nine additional demos now carry structured dataset credits, bringing the
corpus to 65 credited demos, 19 explicitly procedural demos, and two unresolved
ChromaTrace/CHOIR demos. The Tribolium scenes now distinguish the GIANI Zenodo
artifact from the Cell Tracking Challenge source and use the corrected Barry et
al. 2022 paper attribution and DOI throughout.

`Dimension` now normalizes NumPy scalar bounds, steps, scales, and boolean flags
before validation and serialization, preventing array-derived metadata from
leaving a partially written scene. Demo metadata tests also enforce that every
declared credit is passed into scene creation and copied verbatim into any
gallery manifest entry.

The gallery manifest gains 23 additional demo tiles and one corrected title,
with `id` documented as the media/demo key and `dataset` as the authoritative
store path.
