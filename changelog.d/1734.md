#### The demo manifest pins the archives that actually exist

Every Zenodo-hosted demo archive has been refitted, re-encoded, or rebuilt since
the manifest last recorded it, so all 32 `bytes`/`sha256` pins named files that
no longer exist anywhere. A demo would have downloaded the current archive and
then failed its own integrity check.

Each new pin was derived the other way round — from what the deposition holds.
Zenodo reports an MD5 per file; every pin here comes from a local copy whose MD5
matches, and the recorded `sha256` is that copy's. Nothing is transcribed from a
build log.

The set is now uniformly zarr format 3. That was already true of the seventeen
archives the refit wrote directly and of the 546 per-timepoint stores inside the
three timelapse bundles; what remained was the DESI scene, which the rebuild
converts, and the nine archives re-encoded in place with `gsplat reencode -e auto`.
