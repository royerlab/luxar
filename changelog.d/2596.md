#### Viewer: the help overlay explains the touch gestures on touch devices

On a phone or tablet the help overlay now carries a "Touch" section: one-finger
drag to orbit, pinch to zoom, two-finger drag to pan, and two-finger twist to
roll, with the ortho-mode pan/no-roll exception called out explicitly. It
appears only when the device reports touch points, so the desktop help text is
unchanged. Alongside it, the viewer gains a Playwright suite that runs under
iPhone, iPad and Pixel emulation and drives the gestures through the browser's
touch protocol, which is what caught the three touch bugs fixed in the same
series.
