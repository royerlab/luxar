#### Release terminal unsliced ladder buffers immediately

When an unsliced Points, Lines, or Gaussian Splats ladder reaches its final rung, the GPU buffer pool now disposes the geometry-and-texture pair superseded by that last capacity growth instead of retaining it until the byte budget or 300-frame lease evicts it. Intermediate ladder growth and nD slice changes keep the existing reusable-pool behavior, and failed replacement allocations still reclaim the released buffer before propagating the error.
