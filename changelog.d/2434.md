#### Release superseded buffers after completed unsliced ladder grows

When an unsliced Points, Lines, or Gaussian Splats ladder completes on a capacity grow, the GPU buffer pool now disposes the just-superseded geometry-and-texture pair instead of retaining it until the byte budget or 300-frame lease evicts it. Buffers remain reusable while a progressive ladder is still streaming or slice-dependent, and failed replacement allocations still reclaim the released buffer before propagating the error.
