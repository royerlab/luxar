#### Release terminal unsliced ladder buffers immediately

As an unsliced Points, Lines, or Gaussian Splats ladder grows, the GPU buffer pool now disposes each geometry-and-texture pair after its successful replacement instead of retaining obsolete capacities until the byte budget or 300-frame lease evicts them. Progressive nD slices keep the existing reusable-pool behavior, and failed replacement allocations still reclaim the released buffer before propagating the error.
