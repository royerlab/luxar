#### Size an additive ladder's first rung against the resident slice

`stream_ladder` derived its first rung from a download budget — 39,062 elements
for 200 ms at 25 Mbps — and applied it to the node's TOTAL element count. But the
viewer draws one hidden-axis coordinate at a time, so on a sliced nD node that
rung arrives divided by the number of stops and first paint under-spends its own
budget by that factor. Measured per resident slice on the demos this laddered:
human_multiome 6,510 of 1,041,455 (0.63%), zebrahub 5,580 of 640,830 (0.87%),
cellxgene 13,021 of 1,000,000 (1.30%), mouse and ESM3 3.39%.

`stream_ladder` now takes `slices=`, scaling the first rung so the budget is spent
on the slice actually being shown; mouse_multiome's first paint goes from 3.39% to
20.32% with the per-slice payload landing exactly on the 39,062 the budget was
derived for. The new `hidden_axis_stops(positions, hidden_dims)` derives the count
from the data, and the five sliced embedding demos pass `dims.non_displayed`
rather than literal indices — the hidden axis is column 0 in the multiome demos
and last in the timelapse demos, so any hardcoded index is silently wrong
somewhere, and one pointing at a displayed spatial axis would count thousands of
distinct floats.

Two details worth knowing. The count is of **occurring combinations**, not the
product of per-axis cardinalities: `taxon x period` on `biodiversity` is 126 of
140 populated, so the product overstates the divisor by 11%. And the scaling
targets the **average** slice — under a skewed hidden distribution the busiest
coordinate receives more than the budget and the sparsest less; guaranteeing the
smallest would need `n/n_min`, which is unbounded in practice.

This is the authoring half of #2374. The fatal form of the same arithmetic is on a
PLAYED axis, where the viewer commits level 0 only under a frame budget and a
500-timepoint demo is left with a median of 45 splats per frame; that wants an
equal-count ladder (`--n-lods L`, exactly `1/L` of the slice regardless of stops
or size), and the viewer-side half is tracked separately. Reaching for `--n-lods`
here instead would put ~40x the 200 ms budget into first paint and buy a slow
opening frame these demos do not need — two regimes, two answers.

Published stores keep their existing ladders until they are rebuilt; no store is
regenerated by this change.
