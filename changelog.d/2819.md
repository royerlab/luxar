#### Weight uniform batch tiles by occupancy

Uniform `batch-fit` plans now divide positive integer seed budgets using a shared
foreground level, Hann weighting, and the content planner's sublinear saturation
law instead of giving every non-empty tile an equal share. Overlap-dominated
trailing slivers are folded into their neighbor, and the tiling documentation now
distinguishes conserved reconstructed mass from per-splat amplitude inside a Hann
crossfade. `batch-fit run` and `batch-fit submit` now refuse to resume completed
tiles whose recorded grid differs from the fresh plan; use `--no-resume` with
`run`, or delete the stale tiles or choose a fresh output directory with `submit`.
