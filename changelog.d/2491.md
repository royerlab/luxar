#### Additive GSplat ladders use bounded multi-rung lookahead

After the first coarse rung paints, the viewer now initializes the remaining
GSplat ladder indexes together and keeps up to three later rungs in flight when
the decompressed-chunk cache has room. Static hosted scenes reach full detail
without serializing every rung's metadata round trip, while playback stays at
one-rung lookahead and view changes cancel obsolete speculative reads.
