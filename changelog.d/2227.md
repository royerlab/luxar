#### Overlapping blend warnings now lead with the preserving fix

Finalize-time diagnostics now aggregate connected containment hazards into a
bounded, container-first roster instead of hiding later participants or printing
an unbounded line. Compatible nodes are directed to merge and use spatial
`partition=` ordering; mixed geometry or blend modes no longer receive that
inapplicable advice. Hidden displayed axes are named explicitly, and additive is
kept as an order-independent emissive-medium tradeoff rather than presented as a
surface-preserving fix.
