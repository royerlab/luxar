#### STORM demo separates fitted widefield and measured localization splats

The 3D STORM microtubule demo now fits a compact Gaussian-splat basis to a photon-weighted, diffraction-blurred widefield volume while preserving detector-derived CRLB plus antibody-linkage uncertainty for the super-resolution view. Downloads are size-verified, uncached fits require CUDA explicitly, and both views remain spatially co-registered despite their different axis orders and splat counts.
