#### The offline capture loop is six named stages instead of one 676-line method

`runOfflineCaptureLoop` — the code that produces the gallery stills and turntable
videos — was a single 676-line method nested twelve blocks deep, three quarters of
its module. It is now 164 lines that read as the sequence it always was: preflight,
driver and context, overlay, frame loop, LOD settle, teardown. Each stage is its own
file, and the LOD quiescence drain in particular is now reachable on its own rather
than behind a driver, an animation controller and a DOM overlay.

Nothing about the capture behaves differently. The fifty-three existing behavioural
tests were left untouched and stayed green through every step of the extraction,
which is what makes that claim checkable rather than asserted. All eight of the
file's lint suppressions — one complexity, six depth, one function length — are
gone rather than re-keyed.
