#### Correct the neuromast demo's Z anisotropy

The two-channel neuromast demo now fetches the Z-corrected Gaussian-splat pair,
using the measured 2.3084 axial-to-lateral voxel ratio instead of the historical
2.5 transform that stretched Z by 8.3%. The corrected archives are checksum-
verified from their dataset-specific mirror while retaining the published
`cc-by` Zenodo record as their provenance identity.
