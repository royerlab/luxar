#### Capsule joint stencil reach is now something the tests can see

A capsule joint reserves the full disc at a cut end whenever a deficit packet
exists: the deficit term renders `max(mine − partner, 0)`, so the leg's own
profile bounds it and any shorter reach lets the rasterizer chop the light the
deficit rule adds back. That reach shipped correct but unmeasured — the CPU
composition model evaluated the profile over the whole plane, so the clip that
turns a shortfall into a chopped disc rather than a dim one was absent from
the one sweep that could have caught it.

The model now mirrors that reach, width gate included, and clips each leg by
its own footprint; a negative-control test injects the pre-fix half-disc rule
and requires the sweep to chop, which it does at −0.41 of peak against a 2 px
partner. The short-partner bounds sit at (−0.01, 0.005), which a half-disc
revert now breaches on four of the five rows — at a 40 px partner the light
the shorter reach removes (0.0045 of peak) stays just under that row's own
AA-ramp deviation (0.0046), so it scores the same either way; a source
pin checks the packet branch of all four vertex surfaces, and a new
`line-capsule-joint-taper` parity fixture gives the joint alternating vertex
widths so the branch runs on both backends. Note that the e2e layer is not
part of the mobile-only `e2e-tests` job; the dedicated `tsl-parity` CI job
exercises it alongside the unit coverage.
