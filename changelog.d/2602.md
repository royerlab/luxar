#### Viewer: controls sized for fingers on phones and tablets

On a touch device the rail buttons, chips and panel close buttons are now 44 px,
the slider thumbs and checkboxes a fingertip wide, and every text field in a
panel is 16 px so iOS Safari no longer zooms the page into a focused field.
Where nothing can hover, a tap shows a press highlight instead of a stuck hover
state, and the first-run hint says "tap" and "hold" rather than "hover". The
rail gains a Hide panels button that always closes open surfaces, opening the help or the
data monitor closes the docked panels so a phone shows one surface at a time,
and the Fullscreen chip is left out where the browser has no fullscreen (iPhone
Safari). Each dimension slider gains step buttons and a tappable name that
selects it for the bracket keys. A mouse-and-keyboard machine sees none of
this; its styling is byte-identical.
