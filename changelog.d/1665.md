#### The Zenodo record descriptions are generated from the data

The three demo records describe several dozen files between them, each with a
splat count, a compression figure, a reconstruction quality and a licence. Typed
by hand those drift from the data within one refit, and a record is the one place
where a stale number is published rather than merely wrong.

`scripts/gen_zenodo_records.py` derives the text instead: disposition and
licensing from the manifest, per-file characteristics from each archive's own
`fitting/` stamps, with anything unstamped printed as absent rather than guessed.
`--check` turns the same read into a work list of rows that would publish
incomplete. The script never contacts Zenodo — it writes markdown for a human to
paste, and publication remains a manual act.

A timelapse bundle — an outer zip of per-frame stores — is described whole: the
splat counts and source bytes are summed over every frame and the reconstruction
quality is quoted as the range across them. Reading the first frame alone put
`celegans_s1` at 15,721 splats instead of 5,485,103 and quoted its best frame's
PSNR as the archive's. Datasets that list their archives under size `variants`
(the h2afva timelapse, whose record is the whole point) are resolved through
those variants rather than rendering as "no files uploaded yet".

Two further claims it would otherwise have made falsely are now impossible. An
`.npz` is also a zip and a point-cloud `.luxar.zarr` is also a zarr store, so
both parsed as splat stores and came back defaulted — one dataset was described
as having "single level" detail when it holds no splats at all. The reader
identifies the format before describing it, and datasets that are not fits get a
plain file list rather than a table of dashes implying unmeasured figures. The
mirror image also holds: a declared fit whose archive is not on this machine (an
unpulled LFS file, a demo never fetched) keeps its place in the splat table with
its figures absent, and `--check` says how many rows it could not read instead of
reporting a clean bill of health having read nothing.
