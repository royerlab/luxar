#### Stabilize derived encoder metadata across platforms

Companded scalar and per-channel rails, covariance error certificates, and
fitted coordinate-grid steps are now canonicalized at their shared producers.
This suppresses observed libm and reduction last-bit drift while keeping the
stored metadata, quantization arithmetic, certificate tier decision, and grid
snap decision on the same canonical values.

Already-published stores are untouched and continue to decode identically.
Rebuilding a scene can change encoded attributes and coordinate codes, which
produces a new `content_hash` and therefore a one-time cold start for viewer and
validation caches. A stale baked `environment/` sidecar is ignored until
`luxar env bake` is rerun. Integer-step frame axes remain unchanged; the fitted
coordinate bound moves only when its step has more than twelve significant
digits.
