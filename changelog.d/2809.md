#### Correct the stated OpenCell licence

The CytoSelf demo and Luxar Zarr format guide now identify OpenCell data as
CC BY-SA 4.0 rather than CC BY 4.0, making the ShareAlike reuse obligation
explicit. The Tribolium manifest also keeps its non-redistribution message
concise while documenting internally that the restriction applies to re-hosting
the dataset, not to screen recordings or the published benchmark crop.
