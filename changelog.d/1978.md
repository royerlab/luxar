#### Compare scores partitioned gsplat fits directly

`luxar gsplat compare` now materializes the default-rendered leaves of a
partitioned or nested gsplat tree in memory, so the default output of tiled
uniform and content fits can be scored without first writing a flattened copy.

The advice that assumed otherwise is gone with it: when a tiled or planned merge
declines to score itself (no reference volume, or a peak over the memory budget),
the notice now points straight at `luxar gsplat compare` for every tree shape
instead of routing a partition through a full-disk `luxar gsplat flatten` copy
first.
