#### Batch-fit workers read only their assigned uniform tile

Uniform batch-fit plans now pass each worker its exact spatial region, and scan per-frame tile occupancy only when an integer seed budget needs a non-empty-tile divisor. Local and Slurm workers avoid materializing the full source frame or rescanning sibling tiles. Every standalone `--tile k/M` worker now applies cull retention before writing, including batch jobs; a `batch-fit --flat` merge therefore uses per-tile culling as a memory-bounded approximation of one global cull.
