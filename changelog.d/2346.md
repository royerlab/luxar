#### Route hosted demo inputs through the manifest (#2346)

The DESI galaxies, CELLxGENE Census UMAP, and human and mouse multiome peak UMAP demos now resolve their hosted inputs through the checksum-verified dataset manifest instead of reading packaged files directly. DESI retains its source-catalog rebuild fallback when the precomputed scene is unavailable, and `CENSUS_UMAP_CACHE` still accepts an explicit custom cache. The Census demo now exits non-zero, after printing its regeneration guidance, when the default coordinates cache cannot be resolved.
