#### `--floor auto` no longer crashes on a float32 volume with a large offset

`estimate_floor(..., method="mode")` — the estimator behind the default
`--floor auto` — histogrammed the sub-p95 background band in the input dtype. On a
float32 volume whose background sits at a large offset (e.g. a raw microscope
pedestal of ~30000), 512 bins across that narrow band were finer than float32 can
represent and NumPy raised `ValueError: Too many bins for data range`, aborting the
fit. The band is now histogrammed in float64, which has the dynamic range to place
the bins; the estimated floor is unchanged on data float32 could already bin.
