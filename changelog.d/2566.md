#### Sound layer (Phase 1): `sound` nodes, the viewer's audio engine, and a narrated stories demo

A scene can now be heard as well as seen. `scene.add_sound(name, clip, …)` writes a first-class `sound` node — an MP3 or AAC clip stored as an opaque file inside the node group, with an optional nD `positions` array — and the viewer plays it through Web Audio via three's `AudioListener`, `PositionalAudio` and `Audio`, with no new dependency. A sound is *heard, not drawn*: it joins the format contract's `node_types` but none of the geometry tables, carries no appearance attrs, and never stretches the scene bounds. Its audibility is the same hidden-dimension slab rule that decides which points are visible, so `hidden={"story": 3}` binds a clip to a story step and `extend_to_all` works as for points; a clip with no positions plays everywhere, and a spatial clip plays from its place in the scene and gets louder as the camera approaches. Triggers are `continuous` (looped while audible, fading on the slab edge) and `once` (each time the node becomes audible); three buses — `ambient`, `voice`, `effects` — carry per-scene gains from `ViewerConfig.audio`, and the voice bus ducks the ambient bed while a narration plays. Every clip must record its `license`, `attribution` and `source_url`; Ogg is refused because Safari cannot decode it; the clip bytes are folded into `content_hash` through the same payload step that covers overlay images.

In the viewer a **Sound** button appears in the rail only when the loaded scene has sound nodes (click mutes, right-click opens a mixer), the mute and master gain persist across scenes, and a one-time "Tap to enable sound" gate covers browsers that refuse to start audio without a gesture (a kiosk launched with `--autoplay-policy=no-user-gesture-required` never sees it). The embedder API grows `getAudioState()`, `setAudio()`, `playSound()`, `stopSound()`, an `audio` block in `getViewerState()`, and `sound-started` / `sound-ended` events.

The `esm3_protein_stories` demo is the reference scene: a CC0 ambient pad plays under the whole tour and each story is narrated on arrival by a clip synthesised when the scene is built (`luxar.demos._narration`: OpenAI TTS when `OPENAI_API_KEY` is set, the macOS system voice otherwise, a warning and no narration on a box with neither), cached by engine, voice and text so an unchanged story never re-synthesises. `--no-audio` builds the scene without the layer.

Fixed along the way: enumerating a group that holds a plain payload file (an overlay image, now also a sound clip) made zarr warn, which under `-W error` failed every such compile at finalize; the warning is scoped out at the finalize boundary and in the reader.
