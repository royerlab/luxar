#### Shift+wheel roll survives browser axis swapping

Orbit and fly controls now read the horizontal wheel delta when a browser moves a
Shift+wheel gesture off the vertical axis, allowing the advertised view-axis roll
to consume that swapped-axis input. Vertical deltas still take precedence when
both axes carry motion, and zero-delta events no longer wake the render loop.

The fallback is deliberately opt-in: ordinary horizontal trackpad scrolling still
does not zoom, change FOV, or move the fly camera forward. The three slider wheel
handlers that already carried the same fallback now share the central helper.
