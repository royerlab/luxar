#### Playback uses spare frame time to show deeper additive ladders

The default `auto` detail now guarantees each frame's energy-threshold rung and
loads deeper rungs when the playback budget allows it. The next frame's shadow
prefetch can warm those rungs ahead of display, and a fast committed depth can
carry into the next tick. Partial standalone ladders use the existing bounded
energy compensation so brightness changes less as detail arrives. This also
applies during ordinary progressive loading. When playback pauses and the
settle pass completes a ladder, the compensation relaxes to its authored
brightness. A carried depth still obeys the next frame's deadline, and the
loader probes a deeper cold rung only when the prior frame had room for it.
