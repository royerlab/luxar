#### Mandelbulb structure is visible in colour and shading (#1972)

The Mandelbulb demo now colours its surface with a plane orbit trap instead of
the narrow iteration-count band that collapsed almost entirely to cyan. The
trap is equalized through the `magma` ramp and retains local spatial coherence,
so neighbouring folds vary smoothly rather than reading as palette noise.

Distance-estimator normals now drive baked ambient occlusion and a fixed key
light for the emissive point geometry. Crevices remain darker than exposed
surface regions throughout the cinematic orbit, while four full-depth AO probes
avoid the inaccurate-normal tail of shortened escape loops and remove the fifth
probe that measured as visually negligible.
