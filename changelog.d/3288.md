#### A scene can say what its colours mean

`create_scene(legend=...)` stores a colour legend in the scene: a title, one
entry per colour with its hex code, a colour name and what it stands for, and
an optional note. `luxar export` prints it in the folder's README, writes it to
`COLOURS.txt` and to an A4 `COLOURS.pdf` with colour swatches, and adds a
compact key to the printed control-panel sheet. The ESM protein universe demo
builds its legend from the palette its map is drawn with, so the explanation
names exactly the colours on screen.

A legend is capped so it always prints: at most 64 entries, and each field has
a length limit. A long `COLOURS.pdf` continues onto further pages, and the
sheet's key shows at most 16 rows and says how many more `COLOURS.pdf` holds.
