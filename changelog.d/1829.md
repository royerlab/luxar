#### Doctor detects partition split planes in the wrong frame

`luxar gsplat doctor` now distinguishes a healthy approximate split tree over
overlapping tiled parts from planes that fall outside the parts' measured
overlap bands and per-axis tolerance floor. The floor preserves the producer's
halo scale when sparse content under-fills one tile. Doctor safely recovers bad
plane positions when possible, reports a coordinate-frame scale only when
repeated cuts support that diagnosis, and otherwise removes an unrecoverable
misleading tree so the viewer falls back to centroid ordering.
