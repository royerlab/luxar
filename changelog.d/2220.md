#### Partition BSPs follow displayed dimensions

Native Points, Lines, Mesh, and Gaussian Splat partitions now build their BSP
split planes from the position columns displayed when the scene is authored.
Hidden dimensions may therefore precede x/y/z without producing a tree the
viewer immediately rejects; serialized axes remain position-column indices and
continue to remap when nD navigation changes the displayed dimensions. Parts
also stay compact in displayed space, improving their frustum-culling bounds.
`luxar gsplat doctor` now validates and recovers nD split planes without using
hidden stacked columns to replace a producer's approximate spatial tree.
