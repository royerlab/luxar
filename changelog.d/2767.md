#### Preserve fit provenance through flattening and record generation

`luxar gsplat flatten` now replaces partition-slot provenance with one honest
summary instead of dropping the subtree, retaining the part count and only
complete aggregate source and timing figures. The Zenodo record generator also
reads source metadata nested under `part_provenance`, including legacy explicit
frame counts and newer time-first source shapes, so published descriptions no
longer omit measurements already present in the archive.
