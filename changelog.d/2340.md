#### Demo archive rebuilds recover their recorded pipelines (#2340)

The h2afva stack, h2afva timelapse, and two-channel neuromast demos now carry
reproducible recompute paths for their shipped archives. The reusable
`luxar.gsplats.restride` helper performs memory-bounded stacked-axis subsampling
while preserving partition, LOD, and painter-order metadata.

Zipped archive characteristic discovery now identifies standalone gsplat stores
from their format metadata instead of archive member order, while refusing to
mistake a gsplat node nested inside a Luxar scene for a complete fit archive.
