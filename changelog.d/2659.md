#### SSAA respects framebuffer limits during adaptive DPR changes

Post-processing now derives the canvas and every off-screen attachment from one
aspect-preserving, capability-clamped size. Oversized SSAA requests fall back to
the largest supported even-sized framebuffer with a transition-only warning,
while fractional DPR changes use Three.js's exact rounding and update attachments
before the observable canvas resize, avoiding incomplete framebuffers and subtle
viewport drift.

SSAA also suspends MSAA renderbuffer allocation while active, avoiding the
oversized multisample allocation path; disabling SSAA restores the configured
MSAA mode.
