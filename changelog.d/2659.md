#### SSAA respects framebuffer limits during adaptive DPR changes

Post-processing now derives the canvas and every off-screen attachment from one
aspect-preserving, capability-clamped size. Oversized SSAA requests fall back to
the largest supported framebuffer with a warning, while fractional DPR changes
use Three.js's exact rounding and update attachments before the observable canvas
resize, avoiding incomplete framebuffers and subtle viewport drift.
