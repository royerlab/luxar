#### Gallery captures fail before exceeding the Pages media limit

The gallery harness now measures every emitted PNG, WebP and WebM immediately,
warns when a file reaches 20 MiB, and fails at Cloudflare Pages' 25 MiB static
asset boundary instead of discovering an oversized capture during deployment.
Each run also reports total media size, the five largest files, and renderer
capacity drops beside the per-tile coverage diagnostics.
