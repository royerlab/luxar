#### Uniform tiled partitions publish merged quality metrics

The default `gsplat fit --tiling uniform` partition output now records whole-volume `psnr_db`, `ssim`, `mse`, and foreground quality metrics at the archive root. Partition scoring sums the surviving tile-part renders without flattening the splats, parallel tiled fitting scores against the parent's matching (and, when requested, downscaled) reference volume, and `gsplat info` displays the root `fitting/` block.
