#### An explicit `None` in `add_gsplats_from_data`'s `**attrs` now means "absent"

Everything that is not a named parameter of `Group.add_gsplats_from_data` arrives
in `**attrs` and is forwarded verbatim to the children of whatever wrapper the
call produces, where `validate_render_attrs` rejects an unknown key **by name**
and never looks at its value. A present-but-`None` key was therefore not the same
thing as an absent one, even though every one of these keys is a named parameter
of the leaf `Group.add_gsplats` defaulting to `None` one level down. #1471 fixed
that for `labels` / `image_labels`; the rest of the family was still live, and
its worst case still stranded a scene.

`add_gsplats_from_data("g", data, lod_group=True, additive_lod={"n_lods": 2},
partition=None)` — the idiomatic `partition=maybe_partition` spelling, and the
combination stock `gsplat lod --recipe levels` output takes, its per-level stream
ladders being on by default — raised `Could not add gsplats 'child_0': Unknown
node attribute 'partition'. Did you mean 'absorption'?` **after**
`add_gsplats_as_lod_group_impl` had already created the wrapper, leaving `g` on
disk as a childless `kind=lod` group that survived `finalize()`: a scene the
viewer loads and finds empty. The route-dependence is what made it easy to miss —
the same key was fine on `lod_group=True` alone and on the bare flat route, and
merely refused (nothing written) on a flat ladder. `colors=None` was refused on
every route, as a raw `TypeError` ("got multiple values for keyword argument
'colors'") on the flat one and as the misleading `Unknown node attribute 'colors'.
Did you mean 'colormap'?` on the other three. `truncation_radius=None` — a key
this adder *injects* from `result.truncation_radius` — silently suppressed that
injection and then failed the render-attr float conversion with `Truncation radius
must be convertible to float, got NoneType`.

The strip `strip_absent_label_kwargs` performed for the two label channels is now
`strip_absent_attr_kwargs` over `ABSENT_WHEN_NONE_ATTRS` — `labels`,
`image_labels`, `partition`, `colors`, `truncation_radius` — and runs at the entry
of `add_gsplats_from_data_impl`, above every route, and at the entry of
`graft_gsplat_node`, above every wrapper. A `None` for any of them is now
byte-identical to omitting it: the wrapper writes its children, the data's own
colours reach disk, and the data's own truncation radius applies (an explicit
non-`None` radius is still a legitimate override and still wins). The set is
*derived* from `GATE_FORWARDED_LEAF_PARAMS`, the node-attrs gate's own exclusion
tuple, so the two cannot drift; it is deliberately wider, because `colors` and
`truncation_radius` must stay out of the gate's tuple — a non-`None` value of
either still has to be judged.

A present, non-`None` `colors` / `amplitudes` / `cholesky_factors` is judged as
what it actually is: a collision. `add_gsplats_from_data_impl` passes all three
positionally off the `GSplatData`, and on a split route they could not be sliced
per level anyway — a coarser substitutive level holds merged representatives and
an additive rung holds a prefix, each with its own element count, the same
argument the `labels` refusal already makes. All three are now refused at the
entry of the adder and of the graft door, before any route is chosen and before
anything is written, as a `ValueError` (the flat route used to leak Python's raw
`TypeError`, escaping a caller's `except ValueError`) whose message names the
collision and says where the value comes from, instead of advising a switch to
`colormap=` that would silently change what was asked for. `colors` is asked
first, then `amplitudes`, then `cholesky_factors`, so a call passing several is
answered deterministically.

Out of scope, and deliberately unchanged: `opacity`, `blending_mode`, `layer`,
`visible`, `scalars` and `colormap` are pure render attributes where `None` is
genuinely not a value, so reading it as "absent" there would only mask typos.
