#### Content batch-fit tiles now carry their own fit quality

A `batch-fit` content-box worker now re-scores the core-kept splats against the
exact source region it writes, then persists the resulting PSNR, foreground
PSNR, source shape, dtype, and byte count under `fitting/`. The original
padded-crop score remains correctly discarded after halo splats are removed;
the saved score describes the tile that is actually on disk. It measures that
tile in isolation and is not comparable to a whole-fit quality score.

Existing published archives are unchanged and still need to be re-fitted before
these stamps become available to a later merge.
