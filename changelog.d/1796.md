#### Culling no longer leaves unwritable empty LOD rungs

A matrix-shaped gsplat reduction can now remove every splat from one step of an
additive ladder without losing the completed result at save time. Empty steps are
dropped when other rungs survive, and the remaining ladder's indices, counts,
cumulative cutpoints, and rung count are rewritten to match the arrays that reach
disk. Pruning also removes stale quality and energy stamps so the viewer disables
compensation until `annotate-quality` re-derives them.
