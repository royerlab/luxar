#### Hosted gsplat loads tolerate multiplexed slow links again (#2678)

This refines #2644 while keeping its lease-through-body semantics: the
response-body watchdog now follows aggregate fetch progress instead of aborting
each quiet HTTP/2 stream independently. A separate absolute deadline still
bounds responses that deliver no body bytes and true trickles.

Chunk-body concurrency is reduced from 64 to 24, and zarr metadata plus cache
validation use a separate four-request lane. On multiplexed transports, small
`zarr.json` and `.zattrs` probes therefore do not wait behind a full wave of
large chunk bodies.
