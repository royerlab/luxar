#### Zenodo refresh distinguishes inaccessible archives from absent ones (#2368)

`gen_zenodo_records.py --refresh` now reports fit archive candidates that cannot
be opened for hashing, including a failed candidate before a readable fallback,
instead of treating them as absent. Committed measurements remain counted as
preserved, and unverified bytes remain non-failing because they cannot be
checked against the manifest pin. The same hash-failure diagnostic now applies
to `--check`, including when another local candidate can still be hashed.
