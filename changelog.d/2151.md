#### Four compact gallery subjects were framed too tightly

The gallery now pulls back the camera for the earthquake globe, rainbow sphere,
cubic array, and blastocyst nuclei so each compact subject remains legible
instead of being cropped into an edge-to-edge texture. The README captures for
the earthquake globe and rainbow sphere are refreshed to match.

The rebuilt 4D fractal keeps its measured zoom and exposure but no longer steps
past a nonexistent empty slice, and the zebrafish timelapse frames its still at
a representative late timepoint.
