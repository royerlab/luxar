#### Preserve categorical labels through substitutive GSplat LOD

Substitutive GSplat reduction now treats `label_ids` as exact barriers instead
of refusing labelled inputs. Every coarse representative belongs to one real
class, so picking and filtering remain meaningful at coarse levels, and the
shared vocabulary stays unchanged across levels and partition parts.

Explicit merge decimation uses the same label-aware reduction. Its `auto` mode
remains conservative and selects prefix decimation for labelled inputs unless a
merge is requested directly.
