#### Reproducible quality records for the FlyLight and gastrulation demos

The FlyLight 63x brain and Drosophila gastrulation archives are re-fitted from their verified source datasets and carry measured reconstruction figures. The FlyLight demo can now rebuild its archive end to end from the pinned H5J while preserving fit provenance, colour ratios, and display metadata, and the Zenodo record generator explains deliberate quality gaps without publishing measurements from unpinned bytes.
