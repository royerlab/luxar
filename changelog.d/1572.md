#### Preserve integer seed budgets on sparse uniform grids

Uniform tiled fits now divide an integer whole-volume `--seeds K` budget by
the tiles that survive floor subtraction and Hann windowing, rather than by
the full geometric grid. Sparse volumes no longer lose most of their requested
budget merely because empty tiles are skipped. The remaining split is equal per
non-empty tile, so uneven occupancy can still misallocate the budget between
busy and barely occupied tiles; `--tiling content` remains the
density-proportional option. Parallel and batch planning notices now report a
conservative per-non-empty-tile lower bound until each worker resolves the exact
surviving count.
