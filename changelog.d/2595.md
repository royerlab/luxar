#### Viewer: a drag that starts after a pause no longer moves nothing

Press the mouse or a finger on the canvas, hold still for a couple of seconds,
then drag, and the camera stayed frozen for the whole drag. The render loop
pauses itself after the idle timeout, and the controls only apply what a drag
accumulates on the next frame, so a gesture that began before the pause fed
input into a loop that was no longer running. A gesture in progress now keeps
the loop awake until the button or finger lifts, on desktop and touch alike.
