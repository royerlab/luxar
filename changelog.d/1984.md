#### Make partition command failures actionable

`luxar gsplat render` now reads the default-rendered leaves of partition and nested gsplat trees directly, while `luxar gsplat napari` materializes the same selection for inspection. Matrix-based rewrite commands (`partition`, `cull`, `filter`, `slice`, `decimate`, and `merge`) now reject those trees cleanly and direct users to `luxar gsplat flatten` instead of printing an internal matrix-shape error and traceback.
