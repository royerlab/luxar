#### Drosophila timelapse: use of the data approved

Philipp J. Keller, whose imaging the 500-timepoint *Drosophila* SiMView
timelapse is, has approved our use of the data. Because that approval had not yet
been obtained, the record carried an extra publication gate beyond the standing
rule that publication is a manual step; uploading to the draft did not imply
consent to publish. That gate is lifted, and both public dataset attributions now
state that the unpublished imaging is used with permission under CC BY 4.0.
