#### Drosophila timelapse: use of the data approved

Philipp J. Keller, whose imaging the 500-timepoint *Drosophila* SiMView
timelapse is, has approved our use of the data. The record had carried an extra
publication gate beyond the standing rule that publication is a manual step,
because uploading to a draft implies no consent to publish. That gate is lifted.

The approval is recorded in `scripts/gen_data_manifest.py` rather than in the
record itself: Zenodo's `notes` and `description` are both published metadata,
so internal provenance written there would ship to readers.
