#### Warn when overlapping nodes have incompatible depth behavior

Scene finalization now reports overlapping geometry pairs where an `additive`
node ignores depth written by another layer, or where two independently sorted
transparent nodes have view-dependent cross-node order. The diagnostic follows
composed transforms, nD slider dimensions, inherited opacity and blending mode,
and mutually exclusive LOD branches. To avoid pairwise noise from coarse bounds,
the independently sorted-node case requires containment; the additive/depth-
writer case still uses any positive spatial overlap. Existing rendering defaults
remain unchanged.
