#### ESM Protein Universe — twelve stories across 7.7 million protein clusters

A new demo, `esm_protein_universe`, is the big-map sibling of the Swiss-Prot stories tour: every point is one of 7.7 million clusters of related proteins from the ESM Metagenomic Atlas, most read straight out of environmental DNA, embedded by a protein language model and laid out by 3D UMAP. It reuses the tour's story dimension, arrival-gated waypoints, soap bubbles, PDB turntables, panels and narration, and takes two hand-delivered parquet files (`local_data="manual-file"`) that the first run folds into a per-cluster cache.

Twelve stops. Seven Swiss-Prot stories carry over with their vetted facts (hemoglobin, photosystem II, Hsp70, the coronavirus spike, ATP synthase, RuBisCO, RecA), now selected by dominant Pfam family and framed on the densest, purity-weighted knot, because this map is coherent at a hundredth of a unit and a family forms several small knots rather than one blob. Three whole-map stories that only this data can tell — the ABC-transporter spur flung off the cloud, the dark proteome (one cluster in four has no characterised member) and the phage universe — light every member with no bubble, drawing a fixed 300K-point sample so the highlight fits under the viewer's residency ceiling beside the 7.7M-point backdrop. Two families whose knots are sharp here, beta-lactamases and CRISPR-Cas9, complete the tour. The backdrop is coloured by domain of life and dims the uncharacterised clusters, so the dark proteome reads as geography before its story is told.

The backdrop itself is 64 BSP tiles, each a two-level ladder whose coarse level is a 1-in-4 subsample of the tile's own points (light conserved), so the overview and the whole-map stops draw about 1.9M points and only the handful of tiles nearest a knot swap to full detail; Gaussian coarse levels were tried and rejected, since from inside the cloud they overlap on every pixel and drew the view toward the centre at 6 fps against 69 fps for points.

The stories module gains three backward-compatible knobs — the panel's count unit, the overview narration, and the bubble's minimum radius — so both tours share one implementation. Three deliberate changes reach the Swiss-Prot tour as well: every story bubble is centred on its members' bounding box and encloses the farthest member (a median centre left an asymmetric knot hanging out of one side of its bubble), the ATP synthase turntable is the whole Bacillus F1Fo machine (6N2Y) rather than the F1 head, and structures RCSB serves only as mmCIF are fetched as such.
