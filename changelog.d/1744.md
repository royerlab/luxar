#### Scenes can now carry a dataset citation, and 26 demos do

The gallery shows dozens of third-party scientific datasets and credited almost
none of their authors. What attribution existed was in two forms neither the
viewer nor a gallery page could read: a prose block in a module docstring, and a
hand-written `scene.add_text(...)` overlay on five demos.

Attribution is now a property of the *data*. `create_scene(citation=...)` writes
a structured credit into the store's root attributes, so it survives `luxar
export`, a copied `.luxar.zarr`, and anyone who receives the data without this
repo:

```python
scene = compiler.create_scene(
    dimensions=dims,
    citation={"short": "Bui et al. 2013", "doi": "10.1016/j.cell.2013.10.055"},
)
```

Only `short` is required — it is what a tile or viewer renders, so a citation
that cannot be displayed is not a citation. The key is written only when a credit
is owed: an uncredited scene carries no `citation` key at all, so a reader must
treat its absence as "unknown", never as "this data has no author". A malformed
payload raises at `create_scene` time rather than being baked into every copy of
the data, and the rules live in one place (`luxar.core.citation`) shared by the
scene writer and the demo registry, so a demo and a scene cannot disagree about
what a citation is.

`DEMO_META` gains a matching optional `citation` key, populated for 41 demos: 26
real credits and 15 procedural demos marked `None`. `None` is spelled out rather
than left off on purpose — once the key becomes required, adding a demo will mean
deciding whether it owes a credit, and the existing `test_demo_meta.py` will
enforce it.

**Every credit here was resolved through CrossRef or DataCite, or taken from a
credit string the demo's own author wrote — none were pattern-matched out of
prose.** That distinction is the whole point: a citation-shaped regex over the
corpus confidently produces `Prize 1997` for `atp_synthase` (the Nobel Prize),
`Methods et al. 2022` for `cytoself_protein_landscape` (the journal *Nature
Methods*), and `CVPR 2024` for a FlyLight demo (a venue). Misattributing
someone's dataset is worse than omitting the credit, so resolution is a one-off
authoring step whose result is committed and reviewable in a diff, never a
runtime lookup against an API that must be up for the gallery to work.

Deliberately still uncredited, because guessing would be the exact failure this
change exists to prevent: four demos whose DOIs are ambiguous or unresolvable
(`cytoself_protein_landscape` and `flywire_connectome`, where the method and the
data are separate citations; `biodiversity_planetary_scale`, with two Movebank
study DOIs; and `gsplats_3d_tribolium_embryo`, whose DOI does not resolve), plus
a dozen or so real third-party datasets with no attribution recorded anywhere in
their demo. Those need whoever added each demo. They are enumerated in #1744.
