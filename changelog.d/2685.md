#### Existing LOD stores can be re-anchored without rebuilding

`luxar restamp-lod --anchor <fraction>` now explicitly re-derives whole-object
`screen-area` ladders that the migration-only command previously skipped as
already current. Thresholds are rebuilt from the stored level count, the
per-group old→new audit and `--dry-run`/`--group` controls remain intact, and
partition-bound ladders keep their fills-screen `1.0` anchor. Repeating an
anchor that is already stored remains a true no-op, including `content_hash`.
