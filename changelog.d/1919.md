#### Changed (breaking) — one arXiv demo, and it now shows all 3,286,365 papers

We shipped two arXiv-embedding demos. `arxiv_papers_semantic_scholar` could
never show more than about 1,200 papers, and `arxiv_papers_kaggle` was showing
the oldest 15% of its corpus. The first is **deleted**; the second now
visualizes the whole thing.

**`arxiv_papers_semantic_scholar` is gone (key, script, test, README section,
gallery-manifest entry, complexity-baseline entries, built scene).** Its
`search_papers_by_field()` paged Semantic Scholar's `/paper/search`, which
enforces `offset + limit <= 1000`; requests past that are 400s the demo's
`except RequestException: break` swallowed. So `DEFAULT_PAPERS_PER_FIELD =
200000` across 5 fields — a nominal 1,000,000 papers — resolved to at most 5,000
raw hits and, after the demo's own citation/abstract filter, **1,231**. That is
the upstream API's design, not a bug we can fix here; the unauthenticated
endpoint now 429s on the first request besides, and `query=<field name>` was a
full-text relevance search rather than a field filter, so its 1,000 hits were
the documents most textually similar to the *word* "Physics". Delete the
`arxiv_semantic_scholar` cache directory and any
`datasets/demos/arxiv_papers_semantic_scholar.luxar.zarr` by hand.

**`arxiv_papers_kaggle` defaults to the full corpus.** `vectors.dat` holds
exactly 3,286,365 unit-norm `float32[3072]` rows — 2,902,228 arXiv, 308,367
bioRxiv and 75,770 medRxiv papers through 2025-12 — and the demo used to read
the first 500,000 with `pd.read_csv(nrows=...)`. `papers.csv` is sorted by
ascending ID, so those were arXiv submissions from 2007-04 to roughly 2012-03:
a five-year slice in which computer science is 8.7% of the points, because the
CS/ML growth happens entirely after the cut. The "Year" view, which colours by
`update_date`, spanned 2007–2025 and hid it.

Four changes make the whole corpus routine rather than impossible:

- **Blockwise decode.** `np.frombuffer` over multi-MB blocks replaces a
  `struct.unpack` + `list()` + `append` per paper. The ZIP itself streams at a
  measured 204 MiB/s, so all 37.6 GiB of vectors decompress in ~3 minutes; the
  decode loop was the entire bottleneck.
- **Streamed PCA pre-reduction.** UMAP ran on the raw 3072-D vectors, an
  `N x 3072 x 4 B` working set that is 40 GB at full scale. A randomized PCA
  fitted on a 300k-row uniform subsample now projects every row on the fly to
  `--pca-dim` (default 128, ~58% of variance) into a 1.6 GB cached memmap. Peak
  RSS is bounded by the fit subsample; the cache is keyed on `--pca-dim` alone,
  so the 3072-D vectors are not re-read. `papers.csv` still comes from the ZIP,
  so keep the archive.
- **`--sample=N` is a uniform random sample**, seeded and reproducible, instead
  of a prefix. The default is now the whole corpus.
- **Optional GPU UMAP.** `--device=auto|cpu|gpu` uses RAPIDS cuML when it
  imports. Measured on an RTX PRO 6000: **56 s** for 3,286,365 x 128 -> 3D.
  cuML is in no Luxar extra and its absence stays a quiet fall-back to
  `umap-learn`, not an install instruction.

The **Year** view now means what it says. Years used to come from the Cornell
snapshot's `update_date` — the LAST REVISION — and nothing in that snapshot
predates 2007, so the 400,803 papers submitted from 1991 through 2006 were all
painted 2007-or-later and 35 years of arXiv were squeezed into 19. The year is
decoded from the identifier instead: `0704.0001` and `hep-lat/0506004` both
carry their submission month, a bioRxiv/medRxiv DOI carries a full date, and all
2,902,228 arXiv rows decode. Titles and categories still come from the snapshot,
which is the only thing that has them.

Two smaller correctness fixes ride along. Point radii are now derived from the
**measured** median nearest-neighbour distance of the reduced cloud rather than
from constants hand-tuned at 500k: median spacing falls as roughly `N^(-1/3)`,
so the old ramp was ~1.9x too large over the full corpus and would have merged
the spheres into white — the same failure its own comment documented for the
ramp before it. And the 384,137 bioRxiv/medRxiv papers, which the arXiv-only
Cornell snapshot cannot match, are coloured as `biorxiv` / `medrxiv` instead of
being dumped into grey `other`, taking their year from the date in their DOI;
the 68,138 legacy accessions that carry no date anywhere report no year and are
painted neutral in the Year view rather than being assigned one.

The `arxiv_kaggle` bundle cache is bumped to `version=3`: a v1 bundle is a
date-ordered prefix with no spacing measurement, and a v2 bundle carries
`update_date` years. The bundle STORES the years, so changing how they are
derived invalidates it exactly as changing the UMAP would; neither may be
reused. `pandas` also stops being a requirement of this demo — its only caller
was the `read_csv(nrows=...)` the streaming reader replaced.
