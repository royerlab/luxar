#### Unify direct and batch uniform tiled fitting

`luxar gsplat fit --tiling uniform` now folds overlap-dominated trailing slivers
and apportions positive integer whole-volume seed budgets by the same saturated,
Hann-weighted occupancy rule as `batch-fit`. Parallel `-j` workers receive the
folded-grid flag and their exact per-tile seed count, while the public Python
tiled fitter keeps its historical unfolded grid unless `fold_tile_slivers=True`
is requested explicitly.
