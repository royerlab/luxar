#### Viewer: phone and tablet layout — nothing off-screen, safe areas respected

On a phone the control rail was taller than a landscape viewport with no way
to scroll it, the help overlay, data monitor and debug console were fixed
desktop widths that ran off the screen, and panel heights ignored iOS Safari's
dynamic toolbar. All touch-layout adaptation now lives in one stylesheet,
`components/coarse-pointer.css`, whose rules apply only under the
`(pointer: coarse)` and `(hover: none)` media features, so a mouse-and-keyboard
machine receives byte-identical styling. Fixed panel widths clamp to the
viewport, `vh` heights become `dvh`, the rail gutter and every bottom-anchored
surface honour the device's safe-area insets (the page declares
`viewport-fit=cover`), buttons opt out of the double-tap-zoom delay, and the
rail no longer idle-dims where nothing can hover. The rail's item buttons move
into a wrapper that is layout-transparent on a fine pointer and becomes a
scroll box on a coarse one, so a landscape phone reaches every button while the
rail's popovers stay unclipped.
