#### Mesh physical materials, Phase 1: `material="physical"`

A mesh can now opt out of the house shader's light-free, view-anchored key and
into three.js's physically based material — `MeshPhysicalMaterial` on WebGL,
`MeshPhysicalNodeMaterial` on WebGPU — with `add_mesh(..., material="physical")`
and the knobs `roughness`, `metalness`, `clearcoat`, `clearcoat_roughness`,
`iridescence`, `sheen` (each in `[0, 1]`) and `sheen_color`. That unlocks what the
house model could never express: polished and brushed metals, a pearlescent
shell, velvet, and a true view-relative Fresnel rim from `clearcoat` on a dark
translucent base — the marker-shell look the protein-stories demo wanted. The
viewer lights these from a scene environment (a prefiltered `RoomEnvironment`)
that is built lazily on the first physical mesh and never otherwise, so every
existing scene keeps `scene.environment` empty and renders exactly as before;
house materials do not read it in any case.

Authoring is fail-loud: a physical knob without `material="physical"` is refused,
and a physical mesh refuses the house-shader knobs, `blending_mode`, a colormap,
a texture and `shading="none"`, because none of them means anything under that
material. `shading` smooth/flat and `alpha_cutoff` are kept — the first is a
property of any lit surface, the second maps onto three's `alphaTest`.
Translucency is read off the data (an `opacity` below 1, or per-vertex alpha
without a cutoff); a physical mesh is never triangle-sorted but keeps its
`layer_order` band, picks through the house mesh-pick material, and works with
partitions, LOD groups and nD slicing as any mesh does. The Layers panel lists a
physical layer's authored knobs read-only in place of the shading sliders.

Ships with `demo_mesh_physical_materials`, a public
`luxar.mesh.primitives.icosphere()`, and a real-WebGPU-versus-WebGL screenshot
A/B script (`scripts/ab-webgpu-vs-webgl.mjs`, judged by structural similarity)
that scores the demo at SSIM 0.986 across the two backends. Transmission, ior,
thickness, attenuation and dispersion are a later phase: three's transmission
pass does not see Luxar's transparent point, line and splat materials, so glass
would not yet refract the data inside it.
