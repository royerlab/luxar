#### Validate partition BSP trees in the viewer

The scene loader now checks stored partition split planes against each part's
`position_bounds` before depth sorting uses them. Trees in a different coordinate
frame and malformed trees are discarded so the viewer falls back to centroid ordering
instead of silently applying a wrong painter's order. Trees without verifiable bounds
keep their previous behavior, while overlapping tiled parts remain valid within their
measured per-axis overlap band. Existing tiled scenes with a rejected tree may therefore
revert to centroid ordering; the viewer does not repair the stored metadata.
