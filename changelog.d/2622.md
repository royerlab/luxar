#### Story overlays can wait for the camera to land

A waypoint can now be authored `reveal="on_arrival"`: its dimension-bound
overlays (panels, captions, turntable videos) appear when the flight resolves
rather than the instant the hidden dimension changes, so the text arrives with
the camera. The gate keys on the waypoint driver's arrival event, not a timer:
a snap, a camera-less waypoint and a flight the visitor cancels all count as
arrival, a flight a newer waypoint supersedes never reveals, departing overlays
hide at once, and overlays without a `visible_range` are untouched. Narration
already starts on the same event, so text and voice land together. The default,
`"immediate"`, is the previous behaviour; the ESM Protein Stories demo opts in on
every waypoint.
