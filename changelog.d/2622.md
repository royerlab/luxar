#### Story overlays can wait for the camera to land

A waypoint can now be authored `reveal="on_arrival"`: its dimension-bound
overlays (panels, captions, turntable videos) appear when the flight resolves
rather than the instant the hidden dimension changes, so the text arrives with
the camera. The gate keys on the waypoint driver's arrival event, not a timer:
a snap, a camera-less waypoint and a flight the visitor cancels all count as
arrival, a flight a newer waypoint supersedes never reveals, departing overlays
hide at once, and overlays without a `visible_range` are untouched. Narration
already starts on the same event, so text and voice land together. The default,
`"immediate"`, is the previous behaviour; the ESM Protein Stories demo opts in on
every waypoint.

#### Transparent video overlays that survive Safari and the exported app

A video overlay's transparency no longer depends on the codec's alpha plane,
which Safari and every WKWebView (the `luxar export --native` app) decode and
silently drop, leaving the clip on a black square. `Scene.add_video(...,
alpha_matte="stacked")` marks a clip authored as one opaque frame twice as tall,
the colour on top and the alpha channel as a grey matte below; the viewer
recombines the halves in a small shader onto a canvas, so the clip is
transparent in every browser. The protein-stories turntables are encoded this
way (their render cache re-generates once), and a clip the compositor cannot
read falls back to the raw video rather than a blank.

The stories demo also drops its per-cluster "hums" — a low spatial drone per
story on the `effects` bus that read as noise under the narration. The ambient
bed and the narration are its whole soundtrack; `attach_to` sound placement
stays available to other scenes.

Its turning protein structures are now lit by the scene's own baked
environment on top of the studio lights: the same cube map the soap bubbles
reflect is prefiltered into a small irradiance cube (a subtle hemispheric tint)
and a glossy cube (a faint Fresnel-weighted reflection at the limb). The
environment's digest is part of the turntable cache key, so the first build
after `luxar env bake` re-renders them lit and a build without a bake keeps the
plain studio look.
