#### Zenodo records measure the archive generation they serve

When more than one local copy of a demo archive exists, the Zenodo record
generator now measures the copy matching the manifest pin instead of whichever
copy appears first. This prevents stale repo bytes from supplying published
figures or hiding a readable pinned cache copy, while retaining the first local
copy that can be read as the pre-refresh fallback when the pinned generation is
not present.
