#### Mesh imports no longer frame vertices outside the surface

The default welded `luxar mesh import` path now removes vertices that no surviving
triangle references, remaps the index buffer, and slices normals and colours through
the same compaction. Non-surface points can no longer inflate scene bounds, camera
framing, or the picking ordinal range. `--no-weld` remains the explicit opt-out and
keeps the vertex list produced by the reader, including unreferenced points.
