#### Right-click orbits the camera again in an exported native app

Right-dragging inside a `luxar export --native macos` bundle popped the system "Copy Image / Share Image…" sheet instead of turning the scene. Both controls implementations do suppress the native menu, but only on the canvas they listen to — which is enough in Chromium, where the scene overlays floating above the canvas are `pointer-events: none` and the hit test walks past them to the canvas. WebKit resolves the context-menu target geometrically instead, so inside a WKWebView the target is whichever overlay sits under the cursor: a turntable's matte canvas, its video, a logo image. None of those is the canvas, so nothing suppressed the menu and the camera never moved.

The viewer now claims the secondary click across every surface it mounts, through one delegated listener on the viewer container rather than a handler per overlay. Measured in a shipped bundle, this closes the leak on the scene overlays (text, HTML, image and video), the turntable mattes, the data monitor, the dimension sliders and the GUI panel; the canvas, control rail and layers panel were already covered. Text fields keep their native menu, since it is the only way to paste, and an embedder's own DOM is untouched — suppression applies to the canvas and to elements carrying a `luxar-` class, not to everything sharing the container.
