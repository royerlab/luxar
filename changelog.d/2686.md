#### Animated slices prefetch their next chunk boundary

Predictive Points, Lines, and Gaussian-splat loads now use the arrays' actual
first-axis chunk shapes and the spatial index's hidden-axis bounds to warm the
next chunk boundary during playback. Large hosted chunks therefore start
loading while the preceding frames are still playing instead of only one frame
before the boundary; reverse playback follows the corresponding prior boundary.

Loaders without usable chunk metadata keep the previous one-step prefetch, and
multi-axis scrubs deliberately avoid speculative fan-out.
