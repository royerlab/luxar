#### Animated slices prefetch their next chunk boundary

Predictive flat spatial-index Points, Lines, and Gaussian-splat loads now use
the arrays' actual first-axis chunk shapes and the spatial index's hidden-axis
bounds to warm the nearest next chunk boundary during playback. Large hosted
chunks therefore start loading while the preceding frames are still playing
instead of only one frame before the boundary; reverse playback follows the
corresponding prior boundary. Progressive and laddered loaders retain their own
prefetch paths.

Loaders without usable chunk metadata keep the previous one-step prefetch, and
multi-axis scrubs deliberately avoid speculative fan-out.

`luxar optimise` now also reports a per-node warning when the planned layout
would put more than two actively played frames in each of multiple chunks on an
un-laddered spatial-index node. The diagnostic derives frame span from the
stored index bounds, stays silent for keypress-only axes and ladders, and treats
a whole array in one chunk as benign.
