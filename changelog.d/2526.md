#### The `core` ↔ `io` import cycle is gone, and cannot come back

`luxar.core` and `luxar.io` were mutually dependent. The `core` → `io` direction ran
through fourteen function-local imports reaching into the *private* `io._compiler`
tree — and being function-local is exactly what hid it, since mypy does not follow
deferred imports for cycle purposes and neither existing import-linter contract looked
at this pair.

The shared code has moved to where both callers can reach it without either depending
on the other. Eighteen pre-write validators — the gates a geometry writer and the
pre-split path in `core.group.compositing` must run *identically*, or a wrong-length
channel rides the per-part slicer into every part — now live in `luxar.validation.writing`.
The `json_safe_value` attr-coercion helper, needed by `core`, `io` and `gsplats` alike,
moved to `luxar.typing_utils.json_safe`. Nothing changed about what any of them do.

A new `core must not import io` import-linter contract makes it permanent. It was
verified by reintroducing the cycle in both forms — a module-level import and the
deferred, inside-a-function form that hid the original — and watching it fail on each.
