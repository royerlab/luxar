#### LOD selection switches to screen-area occupancy (`selector: "screen-area"`)

Derived LOD ladders now switch levels by the portion of the screen the node
actually occupies. Every auto-derived `kind=lod` ladder stamps the new
`selector: "screen-area"`, under which each child's `coverage_fraction` is a
literal screen-area fraction (the node's projected bounding rect over the
viewport area): the finest level holds while the node covers at least half the
screen, and each halving of occupied area steps one level coarser
(`[0, …, 1/8, 1/4, 1/2]`; a partition tile anchors at 1.0 — the tile alone
filling the screen). This replaces the projected-diagonal metric for derived
ladders, which held the most expensive level across nearly the whole usable
zoom range on dense sub-pixel data — no single diagonal anchor can serve both
the opening-framing guarantee and prompt zoom-out coarsening, while the two
separate cleanly in area. The metric is built from NDC fractions, so selection
is identical on any monitor.

Existing stores and explicitly authored `coverage_fractions=[...]` lists keep
the legacy `selector: "coverage"` diagonal semantics and render unchanged; the
viewer supports both, and round-trips preserve the selector together with the
thresholds it describes. The standalone `.gsplats.zarr` format version moves
to v3.4 for the new selector vocabulary (every v3.3 store is also a valid v3.4
store); `gsplat migrate-format` and `gsplat transform`'s re-derivation emit
the new selector. The zebrahub velocity-streamlines demo gains a
`--streamline-lod` flag that authors its streamlines as a 4-level ladder
(three gaussian-impression levels under the full lines set) to exercise the
new selection end to end.
