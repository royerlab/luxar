#### Verify KTX2 encoder output before publishing it

KTX2 authoring now requires `toktx` 4.1.0 or newer and verifies that the
produced container carries the requested BasisLZ or Zstandard supercompression
scheme and DFD colour model. A binary that ignores an encoder flag, reports an
unusable version, or writes a malformed container now fails authoring instead
of silently publishing bytes for a different codec.
