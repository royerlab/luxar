#### Both ESM protein tours now breathe

Under the existing turntable, the camera in `esm_protein_universe` and
`esm3_protein_stories` slowly dollies in and out — ±95% of the viewing distance
over about a minute. Both maps read as a volume rather than a flat cloud, and
the near extreme brings the knots close enough to read without anyone touching
the controls. It rides with the turntable, so `--no-auto-rotate` still gives a
completely still camera.

The amplitude is at its cap, which is a choice with a price: screen area goes as
the inverse square of distance, so the near extreme pulls finer LOD levels in —
measured elsewhere at 2.29M resident elements against 118k for a modest swing.
A kiosk serving from local disk absorbs that. The hosted copies pay for it in
requests, so both demos now carry a comment naming this amplitude as the first
number to bring down if a hosted build ever feels heavy.
