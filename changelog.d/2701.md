#### Both ESM protein tours now breathe

Under the existing turntable, the camera in `esm_protein_universe` and
`esm3_protein_stories` slowly dollies in and out over about a minute. Both maps
read as a volume rather than a flat cloud, and the near extreme brings the knots
close enough to read without anyone touching the controls. It rides with the
turntable, so `--no-auto-rotate` still gives a completely still camera.

The stories kiosk keeps the tuned ±95% swing. The universe tour uses a gentler
20% amplitude in its hosted/laptop build and restores 95% with `--high-quality`:
that scene has selectable LOD groups, so the near extreme can load finer levels,
while the stories scene's additive stream ladders keep their elements resident.
