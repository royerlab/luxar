#### Fitting provenance survives tiled and batch merges

Gaussian splat fits now save a bounded provenance record with the effective
settings, command, environment, stage timings, optimization history, and cull
and prune outcomes. Tiled and batch outputs keep each fit's record with its
spatial slot and fitted voxel region, with curves reduced to 32 samples per part.
`luxar gsplat info --provenance` summarizes fit stages, slow tiles, stop reasons,
reductions, and quality spread; batch manifests
also retain measured task times and local attempt outcomes.
