#### Tribolium embryo demo renders volumetrically

The `gsplats_3d_tribolium_embryo` demo composited its splats with `normal`
alpha-over, which gave the embryo the look of a shell of surface peaks rather than
a solid body. It now uses `volumetric` emission–absorption, where absorption
(kappa 3.13, well above the 1.0 identity) supplies the front-to-back depth cueing
and the tissue reads as dense.

Volumetric integrates emission along every ray, and this light-sheet volume carries
a heavy diffuse background, so the layer's own opacity comes down to 0.06 to keep
that integral in range — absorption, not brightness, does the shaping. Absorption
and opacity are therefore a single dialled-in setting rather than two independent
knobs, and the display window (0–1.085, a slight roll-down on this direct-colour
node) moves the brightest accumulated cores a little farther into Neutral's
shoulder. The scene's `Neutral` / `exposure=1.97` pin is unchanged.
