#### Correct the particle-collision track physics

The static and animated collision demos now share one charged-track sampler
whose initial tangent, helix pitch, and charge-dependent turn direction follow
the generated particle momentum. Tight low-momentum tracks use bounded angular
sampling instead of an artificial radius floor, while retaining the documented
2x rendered-radius scale for readability. Their on-screen particle legend is
also generated from the same color table used to draw the tracks.
