#### Demo captions now carry compact attribution

Every bundled demo scene now writes the same bottom-right caption overlay, and
credited demos append a compact reference so attribution survives screenshots.
The citation contract gains an optional, authoring-time-validated `ref` field
for cases where the full `short` byline does not fit the caption; references
longer than 40 characters are refused rather than truncated.

An AST guard keeps every authored scene on the shared caption helper, rejects
hand-authored bottom-right overlays, and verifies that every cited demo has a
footer-sized reference. Existing generated demo stores remain unchanged until
the batched regeneration tracked in #1879.
