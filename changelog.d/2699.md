#### Measure sliced additive commits at the resident slice

The built-demo ladder gate now applies its absolute commit cap to the busiest
resident slice of a barrier-ordered level, using the stored chunk bounds rather
than decoding every coordinate row. Unsliced leaves, leaves without proven
slice ordering, and `extend_to_all` nodes keep the node-level cap.

The demo `stream_ladder` policy uses the matching slice-scaled authoring ceiling,
so large time-sliced nodes can keep the required first-rung share instead of
being forced into partitions solely by a whole-node commit count the viewer
never loads at once.
