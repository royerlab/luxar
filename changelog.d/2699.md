#### Measure sliced additive commits at the resident slice

The built-demo ladder gate now applies its absolute commit cap to the
conservative largest per-coordinate fetch of a barrier-ordered level, using a
weighted overlap sweep over the stored chunk bounds rather than decoding every
coordinate row. Boundary chunks that straddle two slice values count toward
both, matching the viewer's fetch. Unsliced leaves, leaves without proven
one-axis slice ordering, and `extend_to_all` nodes keep the node-level cap.

The demo `stream_ladder` policy uses the matching slice-scaled authoring ceiling,
so large time-sliced nodes can keep the required first-rung share instead of
being forced into partitions solely by a whole-node commit count the viewer
never loads at once.
