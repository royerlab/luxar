#### Keep fitting config bundles faithful and composable

`OptimConfig`, `LossConfig`, and `ConstraintConfig` can now be unpacked directly
into `fit_gaussian_splats`, individually or together. Their defaults match the
fitter's public signature, including the tuned scheduler settings and the
minimum splat-width constraint, and each fitter keyword belongs to only one
bundle.

The published gsplat format references also consistently identify v3.4 as the
current readable and writable format, including the `screen-area` selector
produced when legacy LOD metadata is migrated.
