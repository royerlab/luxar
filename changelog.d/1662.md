#### Datasets say what they were fitted from, at the level where the comparison is honest

The compression figure on a Zenodo record has two halves: against the raw
voxels of the source grid, and against that source *as stored*. The first is
stamped per archive at fit time. The second cannot be, because a dataset is
often several archives — one per channel — fitted from a single file, and only
the dataset as a whole is comparable to the acquisition as a whole.

So the manifest now carries a dataset-level `acquisition` block: what the
source actually is, and either its stored size or the reason no honest ratio
exists. Four datasets take the second route, each for a different mechanism:
`cmu1_pathology` reads one level of a multi-level slide pyramid and resizes it,
`ct_totalsegmentator` repacks a multi-scan subset, `visible_human_head`
converts colour photographs to a greyscale volume, and `nexrad_supercell`
re-grids one moment out of polar sweeps carrying several. In each case the
stored size measures something other than compression, and quoting it would
repeat an error already corrected once.

A gate enforces it: every Zenodo-hosted gsplat dataset either declares an
acquisition or appears on a shrinking list, a `comparable: true` promise must
produce a positive `stored_bytes`, and an incomparable one must argue its case
and must not carry a denominator that could get quoted anyway. The selector
works by bucket rather than by name, so a dataset promoted from
`local-compute` to `zenodo` joins the hosted set and fails the gate on its own.
