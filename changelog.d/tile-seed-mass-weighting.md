#### Uniform tiled fits split the seed budget by intensity mass, not a foreground threshold

An integer `--seeds K` on a uniform tiled fit is divided across the tiles, and until now a tile's weight came from the count of its voxels above 10% of the volume's intensity ceiling. On a min-max-normalised light-sheet embryo whose maximum is a single hot spot, only 14% of voxels clear that cut while holding 36% of the intensity, so whole tiles of dim nuclei had no "foreground" voxel and fell to the allocator's smallest positive weight: on an 81-tile grid, 20 tiles holding 18% of the intensity received 0.2% of a 32,000-seed budget, 27 tiles got fewer than 10 seeds, and the tiled fit lost 7-14 dB against the monolithic one where an equal split lost 1-4 dB.

Each tile now weighs `Hann voxels x (mean above-floor intensity / ceiling)^alpha`, its Hann-weighted intensity mass above the fitter's resolved floor saturated by `--saturation-exponent`. The rule is threshold-free, so a dim tile of real structure is budgeted in proportion to what it holds; equal-density content still gets equal splat density in tiles of different sizes, and between equal-size tiles the budgets stand in the ratio of their masses to the power `alpha`. An empty tile still gets nothing. As a backstop, a tile holding at least a quarter of the equal mass share is never budgeted below a quarter of the equal seed share (`MIN_TILE_SEED_SHARE_FRACTION`, water-filled so the guarantee is exact before integer rounding). On the embryo grid above the 20 no-foreground tiles now receive 18.3% of the seeds and the minimum per-tile budget rises from 1 to 46; a 16-tile grid whose centre tile held 6.2% of the intensity moves that tile from 38 to about 1,400 seeds. The same rule runs in `fit --tiling uniform`, the `-j N` parent and `batch-fit` planning, which previously disagreed on the threshold basis; `uniform_tile_occupancy_weights` and `tile_occupancy_weight` take `intensity_scale` (the ceiling above the floor) in place of `signal_threshold`.
