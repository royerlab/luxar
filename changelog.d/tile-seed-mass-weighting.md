#### Uniform tiled fits split the seed budget by intensity mass, not a foreground threshold

An integer `--seeds K` on a uniform tiled fit is divided across the tiles, and until now a tile's weight came from the count of its voxels above 10% of the volume's intensity ceiling. On a min-max-normalised light-sheet embryo whose maximum is a single hot spot, only 14% of voxels clear that cut while holding 36% of the intensity, so whole tiles of dim nuclei had no "foreground" voxel and fell to the allocator's smallest positive weight: on an 81-tile grid, 20 tiles holding 18% of the intensity received 0.2% of a 32,000-seed budget, 27 tiles got fewer than 10 seeds, and the tiled fit lost 7-14 dB against the monolithic one where an equal split lost 1-4 dB.

Each tile now starts from `Hann voxels x (mean above-floor intensity / ceiling)^alpha`, its Hann-weighted intensity mass above the fitter's resolved floor saturated by `--saturation-exponent`. The historical 10%-of-ceiling foreground weights are retained only when every normalized foreground-weight share is within two percentage points of its mass-weight share and tiles with at most one Hann-weighted above-threshold voxel hide at most 10% of the total intensity mass below the threshold, preserving the sparse blastocyst gain while falling back to mass when a hot voxel makes the threshold mis-rank dim structure. One batch plan uses the foreground rule only when every selected `(t, c)` slice qualifies. In mass mode, a tile holding at least a quarter of the equal mass share is never budgeted below a quarter of the equal seed share (`MIN_TILE_SEED_SHARE_FRACTION`, water-filled so the guarantee is exact before integer rounding). On the embryo grid above the 20 no-foreground tiles now receive 18.3% of the seeds and the minimum per-tile budget rises from 1 to 46; a 16-tile grid whose centre tile held 6.2% of the intensity moves that tile from 38 to about 1,400 seeds. The same adaptive rule runs in `fit --tiling uniform`, the `-j N` parent and `batch-fit` planning; `uniform_tile_occupancy_weights` takes `intensity_scale` (the ceiling above the floor) in place of `signal_threshold`.

A resumed batch keeps the seed counts already assigned to completed tiles. If its
manifest predates this change, the resume warning means the finished dataset can
contain the historical threshold rule in those tiles and the adaptive rule in
newly fitted tiles; use `--no-resume` (or a fresh submit output directory) to
refit one consistent allocation.
