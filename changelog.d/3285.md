#### Dimension playback can run as a slideshow, and the protein tour fits a 48:9 wall

The Speed menu of a dimension slider now toggles between frames per second and
a period, from 1 s to 10 min per step, so a dimension can advance every 20 s
instead of several times a second. Python authors the same rate with
`AnimationConfig(period_s=20)`, which cannot be combined with `target_fps`; a
captured viewer state writes `period_s` back when the slider is in period view.

Playback with a period of 2 s or longer is a slideshow. Each step loads like a
manual step and refines to full detail, rather than taking the per-frame LOD
budget of frame-paced playback, and a continuous dimension advances by its
keyboard step instead of a jump scaled to the rate. Moving the dimension to
another position restarts the period, whether from the slider, the keyboard or
a control panel, and on a `reveal="on_arrival"` waypoint the period counts from
when the camera lands.

The ESM protein universe demo gains `--aspect W/H` (also `7200x1360`), which
lays the turntable and story panel out on either side of the map with a
measured text column of about nine words per line, and refuses a screen too
narrow for a readable panel. It also gains `--text-scale` and
`--auto-advance SECONDS`, which steps through the stories on its own and
defaults to 20 s for `--kiosk` builds (`--auto-advance 0` turns it off).
Without `--aspect` the layout is unchanged.
