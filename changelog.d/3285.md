#### Dimension playback can run as a slideshow, and the protein tour fits a 48:9 wall

The Speed menu of a dimension slider now toggles between frames per second and
a period. The period view has one row of presets (1 s to 2 min per step), and
both views have a Custom field: a number and a unit, seconds to days per step
or steps per second to per day, from 120 a second to one step every 30 days.
A dimension can advance every 20 s instead of several times a second. Python
authors the same rate with `AnimationConfig(period_s=20)`, which cannot be
combined with `target_fps`; a captured viewer state writes `period_s` back
when the slider is in period view. The `playbackPeriod` URL parameter
retimes a scene's authored playback when it loads (`0` keeps it stopped), and
`displayReport=<nonce>` makes the page post what it renders with (WebGPU or
WebGL, the GPU, window size, a failed start or load) to the folder serving it,
for `serve.py --chrome`'s display check.

Playback with a period of 2 s or longer is a slideshow. Each step loads like a
manual step and refines to full detail, rather than taking the per-frame LOD
budget of frame-paced playback, and a continuous dimension advances by its
keyboard step instead of a jump scaled to the rate. Moving the dimension to
another position restarts the period, whether from the slider, the keyboard or
a control panel, and on a `reveal="on_arrival"` waypoint the period counts from
when the camera lands.

The ESM protein universe demo gains `--aspect W/H` (also `7200x1360`), which
lays the turntable and story panel out on either side of the map with a
measured text column of about nine words per line, and refuses a screen too
narrow for a readable panel. Without `--aspect` the layout is unchanged. It
also gains `--text-scale` and `--auto-advance SECONDS`, which steps through
the stories on its own and defaults to 60 s for `--kiosk` builds, so every narration (20-43 s) finishes
(`--auto-advance 0` turns it off).
