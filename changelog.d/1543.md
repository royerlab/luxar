#### Camera fitting follows projected scene bounds

Perspective and orthographic auto-framing now size the opening view from the
scene's screen-plane extent rather than whichever bounding-box axis is longest.
View-axis-elongated datasets therefore open at the intended level of detail, and
degenerate depth-aligned lines receive a safe fallback extent instead of placing
the camera on the geometry. This supersedes the closing "not fixed here" note in
the unreleased #1410 entry. Under the default screen-area ladder, some ordinary
window shapes may consequently open one rung coarser than before. The fit still
uses the axis-aligned bounds; sparse clouds with outlying depth tails may need an
authored percentile-based camera and an ``overview`` LOD topology.
