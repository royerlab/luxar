#### Camera fitting follows projected scene bounds

Perspective and orthographic auto-framing now size the opening view from the
scene's screen-plane extent rather than whichever bounding-box axis is longest.
View-axis-elongated datasets therefore open at the intended level of detail, and
degenerate depth-aligned lines receive a safe fallback extent instead of placing
the camera on the geometry. The fit still uses the axis-aligned bounds; sparse
clouds with outlying depth tails may need an authored percentile-based camera and
an ``overview`` LOD topology.
