#### Quantify uniform tile mass-allocation tradeoffs

The tiled-fitting regression suite now measures the mass seed split against equal-share and the former foreground-threshold rule on one fixed haze/blob grid, including per-tile global and foreground PSNR rather than amplitude disparity alone. On that fixture the former threshold reconstructs the content tile best and loses only on the pure-pedestal tile. The suite also pins the brightness-depth tradeoff on controlled repeated attenuated structure: the default exponent shifts budget toward the bright face and moves foreground error to the far face with no mean foreground-PSNR gain. The allocator remains unchanged because a separate biological-volume corroboration did not show a systematic far-face loss, so a depth-normalizing profile still needs broader real-data evidence.
