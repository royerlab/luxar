#### Quantify uniform tile mass-allocation tradeoffs

The tiled-fitting regression suite now measures the mass seed split against equal-share and the former foreground-threshold rule on one fixed haze/blob grid, including reconstruction PSNR rather than amplitude disparity alone. It also pins the brightness-depth tradeoff on controlled repeated attenuated structure: the default exponent shifts budget toward the bright face, slightly improves mean reconstruction there, and reduces far-face PSNR. The allocator remains unchanged because a biological-volume corroboration did not show a systematic far-face loss, so a depth-normalizing profile still needs broader real-data evidence.
