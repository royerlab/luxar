#### Quantify uniform tile mass-allocation tradeoffs

The tiled-fitting regression suite now measures the mass seed split against equal-share and the former foreground-threshold rule on one fixed haze/blob grid, including reconstruction PSNR rather than amplitude disparity alone. It also pins the brightness-depth tradeoff on repeated attenuated structure: the default exponent improves mean reconstruction slightly while shifting budget toward the bright face and reducing far-face PSNR, so the allocator remains unchanged pending evidence that a depth-normalizing profile improves real datasets overall.
