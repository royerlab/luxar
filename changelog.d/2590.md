#### Viewer: tap, press-and-hold and double-tap reach everything right-click and hover used to own

On a touch device nothing could be selected: picking was armed only by mouse
movement, a normal tap drifted past the mouse click tolerance and read as a
camera drag, the element menu needed a right button, and the rail's Home,
Navigation and Performance popovers, the animation settings and the layer
visibility verbs were right-click only, which iOS Safari never produces for a
long press. A finger now has a route to all of it: a tap picks the element
under it (the tooltip appears as it would on hover) and then acts on it, a
press-and-hold opens the same menu a right-click would on the canvas, on rail
buttons, on the play button and on layer rows, and a double-tap re-frames the
scene. The long-press helper is the single opener on every platform, so an
Android device never shows two menus and the button's primary action never
fires under the menu that just opened. Mouse and trackpad behaviour is
unchanged.

The help overlay's Touch section lists all three alongside the camera gestures,
so the only surfaces a finger can reach are also the ones it can discover.
