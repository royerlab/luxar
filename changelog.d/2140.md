#### The h2afva recording finally has a timelapse demo

The zebrahub *h2afva* light-sheet recording had two demos — one single stack
(timepoint 234) and a decimation study at four splat budgets — and neither
showed the axis the recording is actually about. The 51-timepoint fit was
already built, already pinned in `data_manifest.json`, and already flagged
`"default": true` with the note "lighter default for the demo". The demo it was
provisioned for was never written.

`gsplats_4d_h2afva_timelapse` is that demo: 51 frames — every fifth of the
253-timepoint recording — as one 4D leaf with a twelve-step progressive stream
ladder. The stacked time axis remains a coarsening barrier, so no coarse splat
blends two timepoints together.

The Time axis is a FRAME INDEX rather than minutes. The acquisition interval is
not recorded anywhere in this dataset, and a fabricated number on a slider looks
authoritative in a way a frame count does not. One step is five original
timepoints; the demo fixes that stride itself and cross-checks archive metadata
when an archive retains it.

The spatial axes are in lateral pixels, not microns, and this is a deliberate
inconsistency with the single-stack companion. That archive had the 0.40625 µm
pitch folded in; this one carries only the ×4 Z scaling, and grafting cannot
apply a scale — converting would mean a `gsplat transform` pass over the 1.12 GB
fit, changing its bytes and its published checksum. At the companion's
calibration the envelope is 660 × 828 × 831 µm.

Display window was measured on this fit's decoded amplitudes rather than copied
from the companion: p50 0.00050, p90 0.0036, p99 0.032, p99.9 0.057, peak 0.200.
That distribution is far more skewed than the companion's, so its relative
window put p99.9 in the bottom third of the LUT and the embryo rendered as a
near-black sphere. A p90 floor was also tried, on the theory that the dim
majority was haze — it rendered worse because those dim splats are body signal,
not a veil to remove. The scene keeps the additive mode used for its validated
gallery capture because it remains order-independent while progressive rungs
arrive.
