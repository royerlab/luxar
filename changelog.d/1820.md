#### The effective-radii and gsplats TypeScript kernels now round like their Rust twins (#1820)

`src/wasm/typescript/` is not a fallback. Per `CLAUDE.md` it is the PRODUCTION
backend for any dataset above 16 dimensions (the worker's `pickBackend` routes
there unconditionally) and for every browser where the WASM artifact fails to
load, so a viewer can render the same scene through either implementation. Two
of those kernels did their multi-step arithmetic in JS `f64` while Rust
accumulated in `f32`, and the existing parity tests compared with a 1e-5
epsilon — loose enough to hide the entire class.

The divergence was not confined to output digits. `calculate_effective_radii`
compares an `f64` `distanceSquared` against an `f64` `radiusSquared`, so a point
sitting on the rim of its own radius was VISIBLE on one backend and culled on
the other, changing the returned `visibleCount` and the compaction the caller
sizes from it; the discrete-dimension gate could likewise accept in `f32` and
reject in `f64` for the same coordinates. `project_gsplats_nd_to_3d` thresholds
`amplitude × attenuation` against `minAmplitude`, so an unrounded product
changed the EMITTED SPLAT COUNT. Four constants were wrong outright rather than
by an ulp: the degeneracy floor clamped at `Number.MIN_VALUE` (≈5e-324) where
Rust clamps at `f32::MIN_POSITIVE` (2⁻¹²⁶), which regularizes a dead axis four
orders of magnitude apart once `maxDiag × 1e-12` underflows; and
`CHOLESKY_RELATIVE_EPSILON`, `CHOLESKY_EPSILON` and the forward-substitution
epsilon are all `f32` in `common.rs` but were compared as `f64` literals here.
`min_amplitude` and `truncate` are `f32` parameters that wasm-bindgen narrows at
the boundary, which this backend never did.

Every intermediate Rust rounds is now wrapped in `Math.fround` in Rust's
operation order — the idiom already used by `depth-sort.ts` and
`mesh-culling.ts`. The shared `GSPLAT_CHOLESKY_EPSILON` export in
`config/constants.ts` is deliberately left as the `f64` mirror of the source
value (another test pins it); the narrowing happens at the point of use.

Measured against the built WASM module over 20 000-splat sweeps: the display
marginal Cholesky went from 12 651/120 000 slots differing (up to 3345 ulp) to
bit-exact, `calculate_effective_radii` from 2921/20 000 outputs differing (up to
827 ulp) to bit-exact, and `mahalanobis_distance` from 8473/20 000 to bit-exact.

Two residuals are documented in place rather than papered over. `Math.exp` /
`Math.log` are not V8-vs-wasm-libm identical — `expf`/`logf` are a different
approximation of the same function — so the shifted-Gaussian attenuation and the
2D phantom axis stay approximate no matter how the surrounding arithmetic is
rounded. Rounding everything else still collapses them: the emitted attenuated
amplitudes went from 10 492/20 000 differing at up to 3501 ulp to 1649/20 000
bounded by 2⁻²³ absolute, and the phantom diagonal is within 2 ulp (one from
`ln`, one from `exp`). New parity cases assert exact equality everywhere
exactness is reachable and a measured bound where it is not; porting musl's
`expf`/`logf` into TypeScript was considered and rejected as out of scope.
