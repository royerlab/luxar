#### The effective-radii and gsplats TypeScript kernels now round like their Rust twins (#1820)

`src/wasm/typescript/` is not a fallback. Per `CLAUDE.md` it is the PRODUCTION
backend for any dataset above 16 dimensions (the worker's `pickBackend` routes
there unconditionally) and for every browser where the WASM artifact fails to
load, so a viewer can render the same scene through either implementation. Two
of those kernels did their multi-step arithmetic in JS `f64` while Rust
accumulated in `f32`, and the existing parity tests compared with a 1e-5
epsilon — loose enough to hide the entire class.

The divergence was not confined to output digits. `calculate_effective_radii`
compares an `f64` `distanceSquared` against an `f64` `radiusSquared`, so a point
sitting on the rim of its own radius was VISIBLE on one backend and culled on
the other, changing the returned `visibleCount` and the compaction the caller
sizes from it; the discrete-dimension gate could likewise accept in `f32` and
reject in `f64` for the same coordinates. `project_gsplats_nd_to_3d` thresholds
`amplitude × attenuation` against `minAmplitude`, so an unrounded product
changed the EMITTED SPLAT COUNT. Four constants were wrong outright rather than
by an ulp: the degeneracy floor clamped at `Number.MIN_VALUE` (≈5e-324) where
Rust clamps at `f32::MIN_POSITIVE` (2⁻¹²⁶), which regularizes a dead axis four
orders of magnitude apart once `maxDiag × 1e-12` underflows; and
`CHOLESKY_RELATIVE_EPSILON`, `CHOLESKY_EPSILON` and the forward-substitution
epsilon are all `f32` in `common.rs` but were compared as `f64` literals here.
`min_amplitude` and `truncate` are `f32` parameters that wasm-bindgen narrows at
the boundary, which this backend never did.

Every intermediate Rust rounds is now wrapped in `Math.fround` in Rust's
operation order — the idiom already used by `depth-sort.ts` and
`mesh-culling.ts`. The shared `GSPLAT_CHOLESKY_EPSILON` export in
`config/constants.ts` is deliberately left as the `f64` mirror of the source
value (another test pins it); the narrowing happens at the point of use.

Measured against the built WASM module, on the fixtures now committed in
`src/tests/unit/wasm/wasm-vs-typescript.test.ts` (the pre-fix column is the same
fixture with every `Math.fround` stripped and the floor restored to
`Number.MIN_VALUE`): the display marginal Cholesky went from 12 495/120 000
slots differing (up to 3602 ulp) to bit-exact, `calculate_effective_radii` from
633/20 000 outputs differing (up to 1278 ulp) to bit-exact, and
`mahalanobis_distance` at ndim 8 from 5000/5000 trials differing to bit-exact.
The last of those also disproves the file's long-standing note that a multi-step
kernel needs a `sqrt(ndim)`-scaled epsilon; the note is now marked superseded
rather than removed, since a scaled epsilon is still a valid upper bound.

Two residuals remain, documented in place rather than papered over. `Math.exp` /
`Math.log` are not V8-vs-wasm-libm identical — `expf`/`logf` are a different
approximation of the same function — so the shifted-Gaussian attenuation and the
2D phantom axis stay approximate. That is a scope decision, not a wall: a
frounded TypeScript port of musl's `expf` (what `f32::exp` lowers to on wasm32)
measures **0/200 000** mismatches against the real WASM, versus 19 282 (9.64%)
for `Math.fround(Math.exp(x))`. Porting `expf`/`logf` is deferred to **#1830**.
Rounding everything else still collapses the residuals: the emitted attenuated
amplitudes went from 11 907/19 323 differing at up to 1367 ulp to 1734/19 323
bounded by 2·2⁻²⁴ absolute, and the phantom diagonal is within 2 ulp at unit
scene scale (one from `ln`, one from `exp`).

Two limits are worth stating plainly rather than leaving to be rediscovered.
The phantom bound is a property of SCALE, not of the kernel: `phantom =
exp(mean(ln Lᵢᵢ))`, so the relative residual is ≈ `|ln σ|·2⁻²⁴` and the same
fixture measures 2 ulp at unit scale, 16 at µm, 32 at nm and 16 at km — the
parity case sweeps all four. And the visible SET can still differ. At production
defaults (`GSPLAT_DEFAULT_TRUNCATION_RADIUS` 2.75, `MIN_AMPLITUDE` 1e-6),
200 000 splats placed with their hidden coordinate on the truncation shell emit
94 550 from WASM and 94 540 from this backend — 10 apart. The class is made much
rarer, not removed, and it cannot be removed while `exp` differs at all; the
count-equality assertions in the parity suite therefore hold only on fixtures
whose smallest emitted amplitude clears the residual by a stated margin (410× on
the main attenuation sweep).

The parity suite was rebuilt around that: exact equality wherever exactness is
reachable, a measured ulp/absolute bound where it is not, an amplitude bound
scaled by `invOneMinusC` (which is 1.01 at truncate 3 but 8.51 at truncate 0.5,
and truncate is author-controlled), non-zero slice positions so the subtraction
under test is not exact for free, and coverage for every narrowed constant, for
0/1/2 display dims, and for the interaction of a sub-3D display marginal with a
live attenuation path. It is mutation-validated: deleting any one of the 42
`Math.fround` calls in the two kernels turns 35 of them red, and the 7 survivors
are each proved inert at their definition (halving an f32 is exact; a value
stored into a `Float32Array` is rounded anyway; the phantom's `counted` can only
be 1 or 2).
