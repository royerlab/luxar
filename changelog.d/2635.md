#### Background loading respects partition culling and hidden layers (#2635)

Progressive refinement now re-checks each loader's live partition and layer
visibility before every rung, and predictive slice prefetch skips the same
culled or hidden paths. User-controlled layer visibility is tracked separately
from the per-level Three.js visibility used by LOD selection, so undisplayed
resident levels remain eligible to refine. Refinement failure backoff is also
kept for the lifetime of each loader, preventing a returning partition part
from restarting retries and repeated failure notifications.
