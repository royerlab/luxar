#### Oversized gallery scenes now stream in bounded commits

The remaining large point and line leaves in the gallery demos now carry explicit additive ladders, including slice-aware opening rungs for animated and categorical scenes. The 44-million-point 4D fractal is written as bounded 150,000-point parts instead, because a single sliced ladder cannot satisfy both the opening-share floor and the per-commit ceiling. These scenes now paint progressively rather than asking the browser to decode and commit hundreds of thousands to millions of elements at once.

#### Gallery generation now gates on the ladder audit

`make generate-gallery-datasets` treats a LOD-ladder failure in a store it just generated as a build failure rather than a report. The flip waited on two prerequisites: the auditor registration seam that records which built-scene checks gate generated output, and the slice-aware commit cap that made the ladder check's verdict correct on an animated or categorical scene. With both in place and the corpus generators authoring explicit ladders, a newly generated store that fails either the ladder or the scene-credit audit is a regression in that demo.

The asymmetry that keeps this usable is unchanged: only stores produced by that invocation are gated. The second pass over the complete local scene inventory stays report-only by construction, so an already-present store left from an earlier build stays visible in the output without blocking a capture run.
