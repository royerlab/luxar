#### Oversized gallery scenes now stream in bounded commits

The remaining large point and line leaves in the gallery demos now carry explicit additive ladders, including slice-aware opening rungs for animated and categorical scenes. The 44-million-point 4D fractal is spatially partitioned instead, because a single sliced ladder cannot satisfy both the opening-share floor and the per-commit ceiling. These scenes now paint progressively rather than asking the browser to decode and commit hundreds of thousands to millions of elements at once.
