#### Float16 scalar arrays keep their dynamic range when quantized

Scalar quantization now performs its affine map in float64 before rounding or
truncating to integer codes. Previously a float16 input multiplied by the 65,535
levels of the uint16 tier overflowed to infinity, so positive and bounded scalar
arrays could silently decode as one constant value; the same change also improves
the accuracy of the uint8 tier. Existing affected stores already contain the flat
codes and cannot be repaired while reading, so re-author those scenes from their
source arrays.
