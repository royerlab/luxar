#### Float16 arrays keep their dynamic range when quantized

Scalar and RGB quantization now perform their affine maps in float64 before rounding
or truncating to integer codes. Previously a float16 input multiplied by the 65,535
levels of the uint16 tier overflowed to infinity, so positive, bounded, log-scaled,
and RGB arrays could silently decode as one constant value; the same change also
improves the accuracy of the uint8 tier. Existing affected stores already contain
the flat codes and cannot be repaired while reading, so re-author those scenes from
their source arrays.
