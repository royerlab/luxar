#### Viewer: touch gestures reach the camera controls, and the orbit touch model is complete

On a phone or tablet a two-finger pinch over the canvas used to be taken by the
browser as page zoom, so the viewer's own pinch handler never ran and the whole
page ended up zoomed. The viewer now owns every touch gesture over its canvas:
the standalone page declares `touch-action: none` on the canvas and the app
stamps the same on an embedder-supplied canvas (only when the embedder has not
chosen otherwise), Safari's proprietary pinch events are cancelled on touch
devices, and a touch press no longer produces the synthetic mouse events that
would double-drive mouse-only code. The orbit controls' touch vocabulary is
finished: lifting one finger out of a pinch continues as a one-finger rotate
from the surviving finger's current position instead of freezing the camera, a
two-finger twist rolls the view around the viewing axis, and an Apple Pencil (or
any pen on a touch-first device) behaves like a finger for the primary tip while
its barrel button keeps the secondary action. Mouse and trackpad behaviour is
unchanged.
