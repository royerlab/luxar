#### Gsplat adders share the scene-structure controls

Gaussian splats added from arrays can now use `substitutive_lod=` and
`additive_lod=` directly, matching the other geometry adders. File loading also
exposes partition and LOD controls, with `flatten=True` to collapse an existing
stored tree before rebuilding its authoring-time structure; the historical
`lod_group=` spelling remains available as an alias.
