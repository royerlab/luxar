#### Protein-story tours correct their science and narration licences

The Swiss-Prot and ESM Atlas protein tours now distinguish the discoveries,
evolutionary relationships and prevalence claims in their panels and narration
against the cited primary sources. Narration clips also record the licence of
the engine that created them: OpenAI output remains CC0, while a macOS system
voice is marked for personal, non-commercial use only and warns during builds.
