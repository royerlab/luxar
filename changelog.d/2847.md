#### Make `content_hash` portable across platforms

Finite floats in hashed Zarr group attributes are now persisted at 12 significant decimal digits before `content_hash` is computed, absorbing platform drift below roughly 1e-12 relative while leaving exact array encoding metadata untouched. Published stores keep their existing cache identity; recompiling and republishing a scene refreshes its warm caches and invalidates any baked `environment/` sidecar, which must be rebuilt with `luxar env bake`.
